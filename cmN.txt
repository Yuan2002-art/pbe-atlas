Brand cards: plain liquid glass, no colour behind it

The author does not want the pastel washes. Grounding each card on one of
its brand's case photographs made the glass work optically but turned the
grid into a run of tinted blocks -- SOAR pink, Saucony yellow -- which is
not what this page is for.

The photograph ground and the oversized-wordmark fallback are both gone,
and `.glass` now sits on the card itself rather than as an overlay above an
opaque `.card`, so the page reads through it instead of white. That also
retires `.glass-readable`, which only existed to keep type legible over a
picture.

What carries the glass now is what carries it on the home page anyway: the
white rim, the single diagonal sheen and the lift, over the page's own
paper. No colour, no picture, nothing behind it but the page.

Terrex is adidas's outdoor and trail division, positioned around athlete
partnerships in alpinism, trail running and climbing. Its activity tends to
attach to existing mountain locations and events rather than building
destination retail.

**What is verified:** the 1949 founding and the country, in adidas's own words —
registered on 18 August 1949 in Herzogenaurach, Germany, with 47 employees. The

**What the founding date is not about.** This record covers adidas Running and

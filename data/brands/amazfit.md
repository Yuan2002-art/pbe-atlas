A smart-wearables brand owned by Zepp Health, and the first brand in this Atlas
whose product is **measurement itself** — which makes its race-week space a
different problem from a shoe brand's. See `amazfit-hub-chamonix`.

**What is verified:** 2015, and the ownership. Amazfit's own words: "Since 2015,
we have been designing smartwatches", and the brand is "owned by Zepp Health
(NYSE: ZEPP)".

**`founded: 2015` is kept even though Amazfit is a sub-brand**, because the
standing rule allows it: the date belongs clearly to Amazfit itself rather than
to the parent. Zepp Health was founded earlier, as Huami, and that is the
parent's date — it is deliberately not in this field. This is the case the rule
was written for, and the contrast with Nike Running / ACG and adidas Running /
Terrex, where no date belongs to the entity, is the point.

**`country` is empty.** The page names the parent and its NYSE listing but
states no country for either, and a US stock listing is not a country of origin.
Zepp Health's own corporate pages will settle it; they were not retrieved.

`category` is recorded as `wearables`, a value no other brand here uses. The
field is free text and displayed as a label rather than used to group or filter,
and forcing a measurement-hardware company into `road-running` or `outdoor`
would have been less accurate than adding a word.

`positioning` is empty because those lines are the author's readings.

Founded in 1989 by Vancouver-based climbers and named for *Archaeopteryx*, the
brand built its reputation on construction detail — moulded harnesses, laminated
seams — before it built any consumer-facing culture. Its retail programme reads
as an extension of that argument: stores staffed by practitioners, sited close to
the terrain the product is designed for.

Relevant to this research because Arc'teryx treats **instruction** as a retail
format (academies, clinics, guided days), which collapses the distance between a
performance claim and a customer's own body.

**What is verified:** the founding year, the country, and that the brand began
as a basement operation making climbing harnesses and packs — all stated on
Arc'teryx's own "Who we are" page.

**What is not:** two things this record said before any source was attached.
The page says "Vancouver, Canada-based climbers", not North Vancouver; the
company's headquarters sit in the Vancouver area, but the founding city is not
narrowed further by this source, so the body no longer claims North Vancouver.
And the *Archaeopteryx* name origin is not on this page — Arc'teryx publishes it
separately, and that page has not been retrieved, so the claim stands unsourced
here. The positioning line and the reading of instruction-as-retail are the
author's interpretation, not the brand's words.

Founded in North Vancouver and named for *Archaeopteryx*, the brand built its
reputation on construction detail — moulded harnesses, laminated seams — before
it built any consumer-facing culture. Its retail programme reads as an extension
of that argument: stores staffed by practitioners, sited close to the terrain the
product is designed for.

Relevant to this research because Arc'teryx treats **instruction** as a retail
format (academies, clinics, guided days), which collapses the distance between a
performance claim and a customer's own body.

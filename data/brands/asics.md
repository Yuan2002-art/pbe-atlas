A Kobe company with a long road-running lineage, now pushing hard into trail
through its FUJITRAIL and METAFUJI lines. Its trail programme is built around
travelling camps rather than permanent stores.

Relevant to this research for one structural reason: **ASICS is not a partner of
UTMB or the UTMB World Series at any tier** — the organiser's own partner page
lists HOKA in the footwear position. So everything ASICS does in Chamonix during
race week is staged outside the official perimeter, on rented ground, without
the event's endorsement. That makes it the clearest available case of a brand
buying attention at an event it does not sponsor.

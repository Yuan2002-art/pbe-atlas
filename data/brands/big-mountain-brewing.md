**Not a performance brand, and that is the point of having it here.** Big
Mountain is a craft brewery in the Chamonix valley. It is in this Atlas because
it runs a monthly trail-running club out of its own bar, which makes it the
clearest case of a brand borrowing running culture from outside the sports
industry rather than competing inside it.

**How it started, in its own words.** Two people living in Chamonix — Jack
Geldard and Matt Livingstone — could not buy the beer they wanted there, bought
a 100-litre home-brew kit in **2014**, and refined recipes until "the first Big
Mountain beers were born; a Simcoe heavy IPA and a Chinook Pale Ale. These
beers are still in the core range today." It **went commercial in 2018** out of
a garage unit in Les Praz, selling to Chamonix bars and restaurants, moved to a
600 m² unit in Passy in 2019, and the brewery is now in Cluses.

**`founded` is 2018, not 2014, and the distinction is rule 11's.** 2014 is when
two residents started home-brewing; 2018 is when Big Mountain became a business
with premises and customers. The earlier date is the brand's own origin story
and it belongs in the prose, where it cannot be read as the company's
establishment.

**The athlete link is not a sponsorship.** Big Mountain's first alcohol-free
beer, **NANO IPA**, is described on its own site as "Inspired by mountain
athlete Hillary Gerardi" — a brewery naming a *non-alcoholic* product after an
endurance athlete. Whether there is a contract behind that word is not
established here; what is recorded is the framing.

**What is sourced:** the 2014 origin, the 2018 commercial launch, the Passy and
Cluses premises, the founders, and the NANO IPA framing — all from the brand's
own pages.

**What is not.** `category` is `other`: a brewery is none of trail,
road-running, outdoor, lifestyle, eyewear, cycling or retail, and inventing a
category for one record is the drift rule 5 exists to prevent. `positioning` is
empty, in line with the other recently added brands — that line is the
author's reading and has not been written.

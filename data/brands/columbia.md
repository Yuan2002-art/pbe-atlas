A Portland hat company that became an outdoor apparel group, and the first
brand in this Atlas whose recorded activity at a race is explicitly *not* about
racing — see `columbia-hike-society-summer-residency`.

The Columbia Hike Society is the brand's own hiking community, organised around
year-round guided experiences led by people it calls Hike Leaders.

**What is verified:** the 1938 founding, the country, and the origin as the
Columbia Hat Company in Portland, Oregon, named after the Columbia River — all
in Columbia's own words on its story page, which also credits Gert Boyle with
turning the business around from 1970.

**What is not: `positioning` is deliberately empty.** Every other brand record
carries a one-line positioning statement, and those are the author's readings,
not the brand's words. Writing one here would put an interpretation in her voice
under a record she has not read yet. The facts are above; the line is hers to
write.

No `accent` colour is recorded either, for the same reason — the existing ones
were chosen by eye, and guessing a sixth would not be a fact about Columbia.

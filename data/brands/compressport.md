A compression specialist, and **a fourth structural position at UTMB**. The
Atlas already records three: HOKA holds the title partnership, ASICS has never
been a partner, and The North Face held the title for twelve years and lost it.
Compressport is none of those — it is a **category supplier with thirteen
consecutive years of official status**, which is longer than The North Face's
title run and carries no naming rights at all.

**What is verified:** 2008, and Sylvain Laur as founder, both from Compressport's
own history page, which states the year twice. Its about page says the brand was
"born on the racecourse" and — usefully for this Atlas — describes itself as
"unapologetically, a Performance Brand". The thirteen-year supplier status and
the description of 2026 as the 23rd edition come from trade coverage.

**What is not: `country` is empty, deliberately.** Compressport is widely
described as Swiss, and a search summary says so, but **neither of the two
official pages retrieved states a country** — the history page names the founder
and the year, the about page says only "born on the racecourse", and the one
mention of Switzerland on it is an athlete crossing the country on foot. Rather
than infer a headquarters from a search summary, the field is left blank. It is
the most conspicuous gap in this record and the easiest to close: Compressport's
legal or contact page will state it.

The trade source reads as a press-release rewrite rather than independent
reporting, though it is not labelled sponsored. `positioning` is empty because
those lines are the author's readings.

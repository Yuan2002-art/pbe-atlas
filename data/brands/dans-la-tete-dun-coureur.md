**A media brand, not a product brand, and the first in this Atlas.** Dans la
Tête d'un Coureur is a French running podcast and publication — "le podcast des
passionnés de running" — published by **Sunday Night Média**, whose Directrice
Générale is Lauriane Autissier. It sells no equipment. What it has is an
audience, and in Chamonix it converted that into a building for a week.

That makes it a different kind of subject from every other brand here. A shoe
company takes a space to argue that its shoes work. A publication takes a space
because the space is where the next episode gets made — the room is a
production facility and a venue at once.

**What is sourced:** the publication itself, the publisher, and the named
director. The DLTDC House is covered on its own case record.

**What is not. `founded` is empty.** No page retrieved states when the podcast
or Sunday Night Média began. `category` is `media`, a new value in this
dataset: the existing ones describe what a brand sells, and this one sells
nothing on the list.

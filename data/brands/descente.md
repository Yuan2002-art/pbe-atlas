An Osaka company with a long history in ski racing apparel, later known
internationally for a minimal, engineering-led design language and for retail
environments treated as exhibition space.

Included here as a brand record with **no cases yet**, so the empty state is
part of the prototype rather than an error.

**What is verified:** 1935 and the country, from DESCENTE LTD.'s own history
page, which gives both the summary claim ("Since our founding in 1935") and the
specific event — February 1935, when Takeo Ishimoto founded **Tsuruya**, the
company's predecessor.

**So 1935 is the predecessor's date, not the Descente brand's.** The same page
shows the Descente brand and the present company name arriving later. The field
is recording the corporate origin; if a case is ever written about the Descente
brand specifically, that distinction will matter.

**Osaka is not sourced here.** The history page does not state the headquarters
city; DESCENTE LTD. publishes it on a separate company-profile page that was not
retrieved. The prose above says Osaka, so treat it as unconfirmed until that
page is cited. The design-language and exhibition-retail characterisation is the
author's reading.

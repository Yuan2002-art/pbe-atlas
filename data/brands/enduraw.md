**A laboratory, not a label.** Enduraw sells no equipment. It describes itself
as "a sport performance laboratory" doing "data science applied to endurance
performance", and what it produces is measurement: physiological test
protocols, Strava analysis, pacing plans built from an athlete's own profile
and a course's own data.

**That makes it the first brand in this Atlas whose product is knowledge about
performance rather than a claim about it.** Every other record here has
something to sell that the space is arguing for. Enduraw's space *is* the
instrument.

**It began in a bedroom in Chamonix.** The company's own about section says of
its founder Joseph Mestrallet: "From his bedroom in Chamonix, Joseph spent
night to build the perfect pacing plans by analyzing race profile and
environmental conditions." It now runs a performance centre in the same valley.

**The athletes are listed rather than endorsed.** Tom Evans (UTMB and Western
States winner), Ruth Croft (OCC, CCC and UTMB winner), Petter Engdahl (CCC
winner), Nicolas Navarro, Meline Rollin and Duncan Perrillat all appear under
"Elite athletes" — the relationship is support and analysis rather than a kit
deal, which is a different kind of credibility claim from the sponsorships
elsewhere in this register.

**What is sourced:** the self-description, the four products, the founder and
the athlete list — all from Enduraw's own site.

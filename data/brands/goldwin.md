**A brand that named a product line after the town rather than buying rights at
the race.** Goldwin sells a trail-running line called the **Goldwin Chamonix
Collection**, and its own page says what it is named for: "Inspired by the
world's most renowned 100-mile (approx. 160 km) race, held every year in late
August in Chamonix, France." The race is not named — UTMB is a trademark — but
the description leaves no doubt which race, and the collection carries the name
of the town the race finishes in.

If that holds up, it is a **fifth structural position** at this event, next to
the four already in the register: a title partner, a long-serving category
supplier, a former title holder, and a brand that has never been a partner. A
brand can also take the *place* rather than the event — the town is not
anyone's to license.

**Stated carefully, because it is not yet established.** Whether Goldwin holds
or has held any partner status at UTMB has not been confirmed: the organiser's
partners page returned a 404 to this client, and no other source was checked.
The sentence above is a reading of what the collection's name does, not a claim
about a contract.

**The technology the brand argues through.** Voxeloft is Goldwin's own
structural insulation, described on its own page as a layered air structure
that regulates warmth without a layer change, and explicitly credited to an
idea borrowed from building construction: "inspired by triple-pane glass used
to increase building insulation." Goldwin calls the result "an unprecedented
hybrid outerwear piece that seamlessly combines two distinct items into one: a
lightweight rain jacket and a heavy insulation jacket." For a thesis about how
performance is made legible, a brand explaining its garment through a
building-envelope analogy is worth keeping in its own words.

**The athlete is named and the relationship is stated.** Gediminas Grinius is
"under a support contract with Goldwin" and gave feedback on the Chamonix
Collection; the collection's visuals feature him. Goldwin's page also carries
his biography — Lithuanian, born 1979, came to trail running after military
service in Iraq, won Ultra-Trail Mount Fuji in 2015 and the Ultra Trail World
Tour in 2016.

**What is sourced:** the Chamonix Collection and what it is named after, the
Grinius contract, the Vertical Trail Rain Jacket and Pants with Dyneema, and
the Voxeloft description — all from Goldwin's own pages.

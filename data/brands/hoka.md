Founded by two French trail runners and now operated from California, HOKA grew
from an oversized-midsole curiosity into a mainstream running brand. Because its
core idea is visually obvious, its activations lean on letting people feel the
difference immediately.

Relevant here as the clearest example of **product trial** as the primary
experiential mechanism.

**Why this is still unsourced.** It is not for want of looking. `hoka.com`
returns an empty document to this client — its About, Our Story and About HOKA
pages all render nothing retrievable — so none of the brand's own statements can
be quoted. Deckers Brands, which owns HOKA, describes the brand on its Our
Brands page but gives neither a founding year nor the French origin, so it
cannot carry these particular facts either.

What this record claims and still needs a source for: the 2009 founding, the two
French founders, and the move of operations to California. None of the three is
in doubt as general knowledge, and that is exactly why it must not be written in
as though it were sourced — `unsourced` is the honest status until a retrievable
official page says it.

**Where to look next:** HOKA's own story page, opened in a normal browser and
cited from there, or Deckers' investor material, which states acquisition and
brand history in a form that is usually retrievable.

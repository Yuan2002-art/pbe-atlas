**A retailer, not a performance brand — and that is why it is here.** i-Run is a
French running, trail, hiking and fitness retailer, founded in Toulouse in 2007
by people who came out of Sport Pour Tous / Couloir 4, and it still describes
itself as "100% locale (toulousaine)". It carries **more than 150 partner
brands**, among them Nike, ASICS, Hoka, Salomon, adidas, On, Brooks, Mizuno,
New Balance and Saucony — several of which have their own records in this Atlas.
It has been the official supplier of the Fédération Française d'Athlétisme since
2013, and it runs physical stores as well as the online business.

**Every other brand in this Atlas builds space to argue for its own products.
i-Run builds space and lets other brands argue in it.** Its Chamonix chalet
gives a different partner the room on a different day. That is a structurally
different position from a title partner, a category supplier or a brand that has
never been a partner, and it is the reason a retailer earns a record here.

The brand's own line is worth keeping in its own words, because it is unusually
close to this Atlas's vocabulary and should be handled as a claim rather than
quoted as agreement: **"La performance n'est pas une fin en soi, c'est notre
moteur"** — performance is not an end in itself, it is our engine. Its hashtag
is **#RUNSTOPPABLE**.

**What is sourced:** the 2007 launch, Toulouse as origin and base, the French
nationality, the 150-plus partner brands, the FFA supply since 2013 and the
existence of stores — all from i-Run's own pages.

**What is not.** No employee count, turnover or store count is recorded here:
the about page carries a "Chiffres clés" block whose figures are staff-survey
percentages rather than company size. `positioning` is empty, deliberately, in
line with the other recently added brands — that line is the author's reading
and has not been written.

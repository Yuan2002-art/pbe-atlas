**Founded in 2022 by an athlete and a shoemaker.** In NNormal's own words it
was "founded in 2022 between the rugged fjords of Norway and the sun-soaked
shores of Mallorca", by "mountain athlete Kilian Jornet and footwear experts
at Camper". The name "blends Nor-way and Mal-lorca". Its tagline is "Your
Path, No Trace."

**It meets its runners in other people's rooms, all year.** The same profile
lists the brand's public events, and nearly all of them are free community
runs and shoe tests hosted by someone else — outdoor retailers, run clubs,
civic centres — across Spain, Italy, France, Belgium and Germany through the
summer of 2026. Its race week in Chamonix followed the same pattern, which is
why it is the first case in this Atlas filed as `distributed`.

**What is sourced:** the founding year, the co-founders, the origin of the
name and the tagline, and the events list — all from the brand's own
organiser profile.

**What is not.** `country` is empty: the brand names two places, Mallorca and
Norway, and does not say where it is registered. `founded` is 2022 because the
brand gives that year for itself. `positioning` is empty, as on the other
recently added brands.

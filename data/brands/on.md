Built around a single patented cushioning idea and a deliberately clean visual
language, On grew from running specialty into lifestyle retail unusually fast.
Its spaces tend to foreground testing apparatus — treadmills, gait analysis,
measurement — as a way of making engineering legible.

Relevant here as a contrast case: credibility claimed through **laboratory**
language rather than through mountain or subcultural authority.

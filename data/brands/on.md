Built around a single patented cushioning idea and a deliberately clean visual
language, On grew from running specialty into lifestyle retail unusually fast.
Its spaces tend to foreground testing apparatus — treadmills, gait analysis,
measurement — as a way of making engineering legible.

Relevant here as a contrast case: credibility claimed through **laboratory**
language rather than through mountain or subcultural authority.

**What is verified:** 2010 and Switzerland, from On's own about page. It names
the three people — Olivier Bernhard, Caspar Coppetti and David Allemann — dates
their coming together to 2010, places the origin in the Swiss Alps and says the
product is "fully engineered in Switzerland". It also dates the ISPO BrandNew
Award to March 2010.

The page supports the engineering claim this record leans on more directly than
expected: the stated goal was "cushioned landings, powerful take-offs", arrived
at through prototypes built from pieces of garden hose glued to an outsole. That
is On's own account, not an outside reading.

**What is not:** the word "founded" and the city. This page does not use either —
it says the three came together in 2010 and that the company is Swiss, but Zürich
appears on On's other regional about pages, which were not retrieved. The
patent, the speed of the move into lifestyle retail, and the reading of
laboratory language are all unsourced characterisations.

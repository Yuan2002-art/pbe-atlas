An independent, female-founded running apparel brand, and the only one in this
Atlas whose race-week format is sold as **accommodation** — see
`rabbit-clubhouse-chamonix`.

**What is verified:** the two founders, Jill Deering and Monica DeVreese, and
that rabbit was conceived in 2013 — rabbit's own words, which also describe an
outlook "rooted in a California culture" and place Monica DeVreese's own running
shop in Santa Barbara. The Clubhouse Series page establishes that the format
runs at three races in 2026: Chamonix, Chicago and New York City.

**What is not: there is no `founded` year in this record.** rabbit's story page
dates the *conception* to 2013 and never states a founding or incorporation
year — the words "founded" and "launch" do not appear on it, and 2014, 2015 and
2016 are all absent. Rather than promote "conceived in 2013" into a founding
date, the field is left off entirely. Every other brand here carries one, so
this is a visible gap on purpose.

**`country` is inferred, not stated.** The page never gives rabbit's own
headquarters. "United States" rests on its reference to California culture and
to a Santa Barbara shop belonging to one founder — which is weaker than the
other brand records, and is why it is written down here.

`positioning` is empty for the same reason it is empty on Columbia: those lines
are the author's readings, and writing one would put an interpretation in her
voice.

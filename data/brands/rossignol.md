**A ski maker that started running in 2025.** Rossignol's own history gives
the brand's birth as 1907, when the carpenter Abel Rossignol "made the very
first wooden skis in his workshop in Voiron, Isère." Its first trail running
collection came in spring 2025 — "a new era for the legendary mountain
brand", in the group's words.

**Its Chamonix store is a ski-era shop now running trail tests.** The store
on Avenue Michel Croz opened in 2018, before the brand made trail shoes; in
2026 the brand's own calendar lists it as the start of a recurring trail run
test.

collection, from the group's own history page. `founded` is 1907 because the
page gives that year for the Rossignol brand itself, which is this record.

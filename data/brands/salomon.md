An Annecy ski-binding manufacturer that moved into trail running and became
central to the sport's competitive culture. Its S/LAB sub-line is explicitly
framed as athlete-developed equipment, which makes race presence not marketing
but part of the product development story.

Relevant here because Salomon's spatial activity concentrates at races, where
performance proof is generated in public and cannot be fully controlled by the
brand.

**What is verified:** the founding year and city, the country, and the whole arc
this record leans on — that the company began in ski bindings and moved into
trail. Salomon's own "Who we are" page gives 1947 in Annecy, the 1957 "Skade"
toe piece for ski bindings, and then the snow-to-trail sequence of the XA PRO in
2002, S/LAB in 2005 and the XT-6 in 2013. It names Georges Salomon as founder.

**What is not:** that S/LAB is "explicitly framed as athlete-developed
equipment". The page lists S/LAB as a 2005 range and calls it a commitment to
performance and innovation, which is not the same claim, and the framing
argument needs Salomon's S/LAB copy to support it. The positioning line and the
reading about public, uncontrollable performance proof are the author's
interpretation.

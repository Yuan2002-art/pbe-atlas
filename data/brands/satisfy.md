A Paris brand that reframed running away from timing and improvement toward
music-adjacent subculture, distressed materials and a deliberately anti-corporate
tone. Small production runs and self-operated spaces keep the audience narrow on
purpose.

Relevant here because its performance claim is almost entirely **cultural** —
useful for testing whether the Atlas's classification can separate cultural
archive from performance archive.

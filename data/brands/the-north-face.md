A California outdoor brand that grew from expedition outfitting into one of the
largest apparel businesses in the category, and which has kept a physical
presence in Chamonix through its store on Avenue Michel Croz.

Relevant to this research for a structural reason no other brand in the Atlas
shares: **The North Face was the title sponsor of UTMB for twelve years, and
lost it.** The partnership was not renewed, announced in November 2014, and the
title position is now held by HOKA through 2028. The brand still shows up in
Chamonix every race week — without the event's name, without official status,
and without the perimeter it used to own.

That makes it the Atlas's clearest case of what a brand does after it stops
being the host.

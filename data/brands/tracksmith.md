**A brand that designs a collection for each major.** In its own words,
"Every year, we design a limited-edition collection to honor the premier
marathons". Its Sydney 2026 page sets out a race-weekend programme —
exclusives, an "Heirloom Hoodie" chain-stitched on site with a patch per
marathon ("come back for future marathons to earn new patches"), a free
finisher's poster hand-stamped with your time, and a shakeout.

**Why that is useful here.** The patch system is built to repeat across
races; where the same programme is documented at other editions, those are
separate records and the room becomes the variable.

**What is not recorded.** `country`, `founded` and `positioning` are empty;
the footer gives the Boston Trackhouse address but no founding facts.

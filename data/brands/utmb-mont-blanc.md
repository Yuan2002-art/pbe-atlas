**The landlord.** UTMB Mont-Blanc is in this register not as a company that
builds brand experience but as the party that **sells the ground other brands
build on**. It runs the race, and it runs the Ultra-Trail Village — the
exhibition on Place du Mont-Blanc where, by its own count, **125 exhibitors
and 110 brands** take stands in front of **50,000 visitors** across five days.

**That number reframes everything else in this Atlas.** The register researches
individual spaces one at a time; the organiser's own figure says a hundred and
ten brands were present in one square. Most brand presence at this event is not
a chalet or a shop — it is a stand rented from the organiser.

**It is also the party that decides who is a partner.** The four structural
positions the research has found — title partner, long-serving category
supplier, former title holder, brand that has never been a partner — are
positions relative to this organiser. HOKA's naming rights, Compressport's
thirteen years of official supply and ASICS's absence are all its decisions.

**What is sourced:** the Village dates, hours, location and key figures, from
the organiser's own page. Everything about the races themselves lives on the
edition records rather than here.

**What is not. `founded` is empty** — no retrieved page gives a date for the
organisation, and the race's own history is not the company's. `category` is
`other`: an event owner is not a product brand, and inventing a category for
one record is the drift rule 5 exists to prevent.

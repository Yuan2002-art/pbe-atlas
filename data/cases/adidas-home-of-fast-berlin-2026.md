## Description
**A home for the weekend in the KantGaragen.** For the 2026 Berlin
Marathon adidas opens the *adidas Home of Fast* in the KantGaragen — "a place
where runners, communities, athletes and fans can come together over the
whole marathon weekend" (ein Ort, an dem … zusammenkommen können). It "opens
its doors on Friday" (öffnet am Freitag seine Türen).

**What is announced.** Product presentations of the newest adidas running
innovations; panel talks with athletes and running communities;
personalisation and exclusive prize draws; tea ceremonies; community meet-ups;
and a shakeout run before the race. The focus is the Adizero Boston 13, a
training shoe the page traces to "eine Silhouette… die erstmals 1980
vorgestellt wurde" — a silhouette first introduced in 1980.

## Verification notes
**Five pictures, and none is the Home of Fast.** The space opens this weekend
and nothing has been published from inside it. Two are campaign photographs
from the organiser's own news page; two are product shots of the Cromo
collaboration, which are at least evidence of the announced tea ceremonies; one
is the venue's empty floor.

**The venue photograph may be the wrong building.** The activation is reported
at Kantstrasse 126 and the operator's own site says Kantstrasse 127. The
complex is large. It is included as the nearest available view and the caption
says so.

**Revisit after 27 September.** Everything in this record is written from an
announcement, and the pictures are no exception.

**An advertisement on the organiser's site.** The page is marked "Werbung".
It is filed `event-organiser` because it is the race's own site, but its voice
is adidas's.

**Announced, not yet open.** Written on 22 September 2026. The page gives
"Friday" and "the whole marathon weekend"; the record uses 25–27 September.

**Nothing read describes the KantGaragen.** The pin is the building from the
map, precision `exact`.

**adidas has an expo stand too.** The organiser's expo page lists adidas at
the MARATHON EXPO at Tempelhof, per a research pass; that stand is not this
record.

**First case for this brand record.** "adidas Running / Terrex" had no cases.

**No picture stored.** The page's images show runners, not the space.

## Description
**A chalet inside the organiser's village, built around the foot.** For UTMB
week 2026 Altra took a chalet in the Ultra-Trail Village on Place du
Mont-Blanc — "the Altra Chalet at the UTMB® Village", in the brand's words.
What it offered started below the shoe: visitors could "test the strength of
your toes, join a mobility workshop, and receive a giveaway designed to help
make your feet even stronger."

**The shoe test was a loop out and back.** Altra offered "the chance to test
our newest trail running shoes on the trails they were built for." A Strava
challenge set two featured segments, **Climb To Floria Hut** and **Grand
Balcon Sud**: run one "in Altra test shoes, and return to the chalet to claim
your reward: a limited-edition t-shirt."

**Two dated sessions left from the chalet.** *Run Without Metrics*, on Tuesday
25 August at 10:00, was a coached community run: "Forget about metrics and
enjoy an immersive experience", after which participants guessed the distance
covered and the closest won a pair of shoes. A women's community run left the
chalet at 15:00 on Wednesday 26 August, followed at 16:00 by a panel on
"the role of women in trail running" at Maison des Artistes.

**The film was elsewhere.** *HEAL*, a film following Altra athlete Alban
Berson "on his journey with ADHD", premièred the same evening at Maison des
Artistes, screening at 20:30 after a reception. HyperSupers TDAH France, the
ADHD association invited to speak, described it as strictly private and by
invitation only, for Altra's teams and professionals.

## Verification notes
**The brand's own post says "Challenges", plural, and covers the whole week.**
Altra's Instagram of 20 August 2026 repeats the chalet offer almost verbatim
from its page — the toe-strength test, the mobility workshop, the athlete
stories — and frames it as "all week long in Chamonix" rather than as the two
dated sessions alone. The Strava challenge exists under Altra's own title,
"Altra x Chamonix | Climb to Floria Hut", which settles the segment naming in
the brand's favour against the third-party listing.

**The earlier classification of this lead was wrong.** The Atlas's notes
listed the Altra Chalet among the leads that are *not* Ultra-Trail Village
stands. Altra's own page puts it "at the UTMB® Village", so it is one. It is
written up anyway, because the reason Village stands were set aside was that
they rest on one line from an aggregator; this one has the brand's own
programme behind it. That is the same footing as the Voxeloft Trail Jacket
case, which is also in the Village.

**The chalet's dates are the Village's.** Altra's page gives no opening
dates or hours for the chalet — only the two dated sessions. The record uses
the organiser's dates for the Village, 24 to 28 August, as the outer bound,
and marks precision `unknown` so it does not read as the brand's own dates.

**The page is undated, and the year is inferred.** It says "Every August" and
gives weekdays with dates: Tuesday 25 August and Wednesday 26 August fall on
those days in 2026 and not in 2025, which is how the page is tied to this
edition.

**Several things the listing reports are not on the brand's page, and are
left out.** The listing names a foot health expert as leading the workshops,
an evening hub at a bar in town, a branded space at a hotel, and an
invite-only party; Altra's page mentions none of them. The listing also gives
the Strava segments as "Climb to Chalet La Floria" and "Balcon Sud"; the
record uses Altra's names.

**Two sources disagree about the film.** Altra's page has a "Register" button
and speaks of "guests"; the invited association calls the evening strictly
private and by invitation only; the listing calls it ticketed. The record
states only what both brand and association agree on — the date, the venue
and the film — and notes the association's account as its own.

**The pin is the Village's, not the chalet's.** No stand number or position
was published, so the coordinate is the Ultra-Trail Village's and precision
is `approximate`, as on the other Village cases.

**Strava is recorded narrowly.** The segments are on Strava; that is evidence
of the platform, not of a partnership.

**Five pictures, and not one of them is the chalet.** All come from Altra's
own page. Four are running photographs placed beside the page's sections —
terrain, a group run, two trail frames — none of them dated or located by the
page, so none can be said to be from this edition or from Chamonix. The fifth
is the HEAL poster, and the film it advertises premièred at Maison des
Artistes rather than at the chalet, which the captions say. **The Altra Chalet
itself is undocumented in pictures.** Shoe shots on the same page were left
out, because nothing says which models were the test pairs.

**What is not known:** what the toe-strength test involves, who ran the
mobility workshop, which shoe models were available to test, how many
t-shirts there were, and whether any of it happened as planned — the page
reads as an announcement and was not updated after the week.

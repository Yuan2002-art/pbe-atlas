## Description
For UTMB race week 2026 Amazfit ran a house in Chamonix, called in the same
sources both the **Amazfit Hub** and the **Amazfit House**. The agency that made
it, Athletic Affair, describes it in the first person: *"We've created The
Amazfit Hub in Chamonix, that serves as the ultimate hub for performance,
community, and brand experience during UTMB week"*, a house "designed to bring
runners, media, and trail-running enthusiasts closer to the Amazfit team".

The programme, as the agency lists it:

- **Industry Night** — *"Industry Night by Amazfit & Athletic Affair"*, in its
  **fourth edition**: a BBQ and drinks evening at the Amazfit House for media,
  athletes, industry professionals, brand partners and community leaders.
  Attendance confirmed by emailing the agency.
- **Ben Dhiman & Rod Farvard Live Podcast Show** — an informal breakfast for
  networking, athlete interaction and product discovery, then a live podcast
  recording with the two Amazfit athletes.
- **Social run: mileSTONES Chamonix Edition** — a travelling format the agency
  calls *"an exciting alternative to the traditional shake-out run"*.
- **Amazfit Night cheering point** — at the **Notre-Dame de la Gorge** meeting
  point, to cheer athletes through that section of the course. This is not in
  Chamonix.

Running alongside the house was a **data narrative** built on one athlete: Ruth
Croft, who won at Chamonix the previous year, returning with the Cheetah 2 Ultra
and Helio Armband *"tracking the training and data behind her journey back to
Chamonix"*.

## Verification notes
**The best source here is the agency's, which is unusual and worth naming.**
Most records in this Atlas rest on the brand or on trade press. This one rests
on Athletic Affair's own newsletter, filed as `agency-studio` — the studio that
built the thing describing what it built. It is not independent, and it is
dated 17 August 2026, a week before the race week opened, so everything in the
Description is a plan rather than an observation. No post-event account was
found.

**The address was never published.** The agency names the house repeatedly and
never says where it is. The third-party listing supplies a coordinate; this
record does not use it, because an aggregator's pin is not a sourced address.
The pin is Chamonix town centre and the precision is `city`.

**The two sources disagree about who it was for.** The listing describes the Hub
as "open to the public with free activities". The agency's account has Industry
Night as an invited evening for media, athletes, industry professionals and
brand partners, confirmed by email to a named agency address. Both can be
partly true — some sessions public, one industry-only — but "open to the
public" is not an accurate summary of what the agency describes, and the record
follows the agency.

**The dates come from the listing, not the agency.** 24–30 August matches the
edition, but the agency's account gives no dates for any individual session and
no run of days for the house. If a schedule page exists, it would upgrade this.

**The cheering point is not in Chamonix.** Notre-Dame de la Gorge sits above Les
Contamines, on the UTMB course, some distance down the valley. So this record,
like Compressport's and rabbit's, covers a programme with more than one
location, and the case is filed on the house.

**`product: ""` although the brand sells the product being discussed.** Devices
are named — Cheetah 2 Ultra, Helio Armband — but as the kit an athlete wore in
her own training, not as anything released, sold or tried at the Hub. Putting
them in the product field would imply a launch that no source describes.

**What is not known is the room.** No source describes the building, its size,
what was installed in it, or whether it was a hotel, a chalet or a shopfront.
For an Atlas of spaces this record is strong on programme and silent on
architecture — the same shape of gap as Columbia's and Compressport's.

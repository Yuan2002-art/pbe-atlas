## Description
During UTMB week 2026 ASICS operated two spaces in Chamonix while holding no
partnership with the event. The organiser's own partner list names HOKA in the
footwear position and does not include ASICS at any tier.

The **ASICS Trail Pop-Up**, reported as the brand's first, was built around
archives and a designed scenography, screened a mini-film inside the space, and
showed athlete race kits alongside product-development storytelling. Its
centrepiece was the **METAFUJI TRAIL 2**. Trade coverage describes a design
concept drawing on Japanese calligraphy and natural materials, with a lichen
motif used to link Mount Fuji and Mont-Blanc.

Alongside it, the **ASICS Trail Camp** ran 24–27 August, 10:00–19:00, at L'Index
on Promenade des Crèmeries, just outside the town centre: product testing,
expert advice, athlete meetings, recovery and group runs, with daily themed
sessions on nutrition, recovery, equipment and performance, including a run with
athlete Tom Evans on Wednesday 26 August.

**No official ASICS press release** about either 2026 space was found in the

coverage. Four outlets were used, and they are at least partly independent of
each other.

**The date range combines the two spaces.** The Trail Camp's 24–27 August is
sourced directly; the pop-up's dates are not, so the range is extended to the end
of race week rather than asserted precisely.

**No agency is credited for the 2026 pop-up.** An awards submission credits the
agency **Mile & Stone** with the earlier Fujitrail Camp programme, but that is a
self-reported entry, it concerns previous editions (Chamonix 2023 and 2024), and

recorded as fact.

**Separate and not part of this case:** ASICS announced an athlete chalet called
ASICS Basecamp at Les Houches in May 2026. That is a permanent training facility,
not a consumer activation.

## Strategic purpose
Claim the cultural high ground of trail running at the sport's most visible
gathering without paying for access to it — substituting a designed argument
about where the brand comes from for the official status it does not hold.

## Archive use
The pilot's key counter-case to HOKA. Same week, same valley, opposite
structural position: one brand owns the event's name, the other has no
relationship to it at all. What each builds in that condition is the comparison
the Atlas exists to make.

## Why this location?
Outside the official perimeter, because it has to be. Not being a partner
excludes ASICS from the Ultra-Trail Village, so the strategy converts the
exclusion into a position: a separate address the visitor travels to, which reads
as a destination rather than a stand among a hundred others.

## Why this timing?
Race week is the only moment the trail audience is concentrated, and for a brand
without sponsorship it is also the only moment when an independent space can
borrow that concentration at all.

## Why this event?
UTMB certifies who is serious in trail running. ASICS is a road-running
institution moving into a category where it has no lineage, and proximity to
this specific race is the fastest available route to being read as a trail brand
rather than a visitor from the road.

## Why this product?
METAFUJI TRAIL 2 carries Mount Fuji in its name, which is the whole argument
compressed into a word. The lichen motif linking Fuji to Mont-Blanc makes the
claim spatial: this is not a Japanese road brand visiting the Alps, it is a
mountain brand from a different mountain.

## What performance claim is being reinforced?
Lineage and craft rather than measured advantage. The archives and race kits
argue that the development history is real; nothing on site tests or proves
current performance.

## What cultural meaning is being built?
A specifically Japanese authority in a European alpine setting — calligraphy,
natural materials, a mountain of its own. It sidesteps the question of whether
ASICS belongs at UTMB by changing which mountain culture is being invoked.

## What role does the physical experience play?
Decisive, because the argument is entirely aesthetic. A lichen motif linking two
mountains cannot survive being written down; it only works as material, light and
sequence in a room. This is the one case in the pilot that would lose almost
everything in translation to a screen.

## Experience mechanism
Curation. The visitor is walked through an argument — archive, film, object,
material — in a controlled order, closer to an exhibition than to a shop or a
test centre. The Trail Camp then supplies the physical trial the pop-up
deliberately withholds.

## Key strategic insight
Sponsorship can be substituted with narrative: barred from the official
perimeter, ASICS answers spatial exclusion with cultural specificity, competing
on where it comes from rather than on its position inside the event.

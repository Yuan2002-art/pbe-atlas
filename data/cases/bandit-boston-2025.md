## Description
**The brand's third Boston pop-up, at the same address.** For the 2025
Boston Marathon Bandit returned to 250 Boylston Street for "our third Back Bay
pop-up with a series of running events, experiences, and a limited capsule
inspired by the iconic race", open from Friday 18 to Tuesday 22 April.

**A week built around one capsule and five moments.** Members got first access
on the Friday morning, "early access to all collections". Then: a Saturday
evening happy hour for everyone who ran the 5K; a Sunday shakeout, "three very
easy, very vibey miles around the neighborhood, then back to the Bandit Boston
pop-up for beverages and bites"; on race morning, a stop for "last-minute
runner's essentials"; and on Monday afternoon and Tuesday morning, medal
portraits — "Bring those dead legs over for a medal portrait that'll last a
lifetime."

## Verification notes
**Three pictures, and none of them is the pop-up.** All come from the brand's
own lookbook for the capsule, published on the journal entry that announces
"our 250 Boylston Street pop-up, opening Friday". They show what was sold and
where it was worn, not the room it was sold in. **No photograph of the shop at
250 Boylston Street has been found for 2025.**

**A warning that nearly cost this record.** A trend site's page describing a
Bandit space in Boston - a garden, clover, hoses - turned out to describe the
**2026** room, not this one. It was checked and rejected. The two years share
an address, so anything found for "Bandit Boston" has to be dated before it
is believed.

**Six programme tiles on the pop-up page were left out** at 600px; they are
event graphics rather than pictures of anything.

**"Back Bay" and "a few blocks from the finish line" are the brand's words.**
The geocoder places 250 Boylston Street in Downtown rather than Back Bay. The
record keeps the address and does not repeat either description as fact.

**The pin nearly went to the wrong neighbourhood.** A first geocoding of "250
Boylston Street, Boston" returned a house of the same number in Jamaica Plain;
the postcode 02116 settled it.

**Written from the plan.** The page was posted on 15 April, before the week.

**Paired with 2026.** The same address carries Bandit's 2026 pop-up, which is a
separate record so the two can be compared.

**No picture stored.** The page's images are generic banners.

## Description
**The same address, a different argument.** A year after its third Boston
pop-up, Bandit was back at 250 Boylston Street for the 2026 Boston Marathon,
and this time the room had a theme. "Once inside, customers were greeted with
a green, luck-themed interior and Boston-branded merchandise to celebrate the
2026 race," reported Athletech News; the line to get in "consistently had a
line wrapped around the corner".

**The brand said what the room was for.** Bandit's chief marketing officer,
Catherine Wolpe: "Marathon day always carries an element of chance —
conditions shift, bodies have to respond, and outcomes can hinge on the
smallest variables. But runners know luck is rarely accidental. … We wanted to
land the idea that while luck can't be manufactured, it has a way of finding
those who've put in the work."

**What else is known.** Several shakeout runs brought runners into the pop-up,
one with ASICS. Wolpe told the magazine the brand saw "more than twice the
amount of people visiting than last year" — against the 2025 pop-up, which is
its own record here.

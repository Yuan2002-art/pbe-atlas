## Description
**A brewery that runs a running club.** Big Mountain Basecamp is a permanent
craft-beer bar at 365 Avenue Ravanel le Rouge in Chamonix, operated by Big
Mountain Brewing Co. and open every day from 12:00 to 23:00. The brand
describes it as "the ultimate place to share a locally-brewed craft beer with
friends after a day in the mountains," and the room carries twenty taps, a
kitchen serving pizzas, burgers and sharing plates, a south-facing terrace, a
beer garden, a take-out beer shop and an on-site micro-brewery.

**The reason it is in this Atlas is one line on the brand's own page:**
*"Basecamp is the HQ of the Big Mountain Trail Club. Join us every last
Wednesday of the month for a friendly group run, a raffle, and a free beer."*

That makes it the only case here whose occupant is not a sports brand at all.
Every other record is a company that sells performance equipment building a
space to argue for it. This is a brewery, and the running club is a standing
fixture of a commercial room that would exist without it.

**The rest of the week is not running.** The published programme is a €5 happy
hour daily from 16:00 to 17:30, two-for-one pizzas every Tuesday, and live
music on Sundays through the winter. The trail club is one Wednesday a month
inside that.

**Where it sits.** The brand gives the location as "near the centre of Chamonix
Mont-Blanc, just 10 minutes walk from the Aiguille du Midi lift station," with
a south-facing terrace facing the massif.

## Verification notes
**Everything above comes from the brewery's own two pages** — the Basecamp page
and the company story — which is why this record does not rest on the
aggregator that surfaced it.

**No opening date is published**, so the date carries `precision: unknown` and
`ongoing: true`, the same shape as the Salomon store. The brand's story page
runs 2014 to 2025 without saying when Basecamp itself opened; the photograph
used here was uploaded in May 2025, which is a bound and not a date, and is not
recorded as one.

**The coordinate is the street, not the door.** 365 Avenue Ravanel le Rouge is
published, but that address was not geocoded here, so the pin sits on the
street and precision is `approximate`.

**No `relatedEvent`, deliberately.** Basecamp is permanent and the trail club
is monthly; neither belongs to an edition. The aggregator that surfaced this
space listed three UTMB-week sessions at it — a shoe test, a social run and a
CHAM-O orienteering challenge with HOKA test shoes and Silva headtorches —
**and none of those is recorded here.** They are activities hosted in this room
by other parties during one week, they rest on the aggregator alone, and
attaching them would turn a permanent bar into a UTMB activation, which it is
not. If any of them is researched, it belongs to the brand that ran it.

**What the club actually is has not been established.** Membership, size,
whether it predates Basecamp, and who leads the runs are all unrecorded: the
brand gives one sentence and a cadence. The Atlas has a case for the room and
a calendar entry for the club, not a picture of the community.

**One photograph, from the brand.** It shows the building from the street in
winter. Nothing of the interior, the terrace, the brewery or a club run was
retrieved, so the space is documented from outside only.

## Description
**A two-day takeover of London's biggest screen building.** On the Friday and
Saturday before the 2026 London Marathon, Brooks "took over the entire
Outernet district" — the digital exhibition complex at Tottenham Court Road —
as "a two-day hub of movement, community, and culture".

**What was in it.** A retail space "where visitors could browse the latest
gear and even test the shoes out on treadmills", with early access to the
Hyperion Elite 6 and a limited-edition collaboration with the label Boys Get
Sad Too; a "Get Ready With Brooks" station with nail art, temporary tattoos
and hair braiding; and DJ sets closing each day.

**Who ran it.** Joe Wicks led workouts on the Friday; Boys Get Sad Too hosted a
community run from Outernet that evening; the Nobody Asked Us podcast recorded
live with Cynthia Erivo, Des Linden and Kara Goucher — a "private podcast
event for 300 guests", per the venue. On the Saturday: a Brooks Run Club
shakeout with Linden and Goucher, and two parkruns led by ambassadors Ashley
Cain and Katie Taylor.

**On race day it left the building.** Cheer zones at The Pacific Tavern in
Canada Water and at Victoria Station. Those are not this record.

## Verification notes
**No Brooks page could be read.** brooksrunning.com returned 403. The record
rests on the venue operator's own case study, written after the event, and on
a listings magazine written before it.

**Two days, not three.** The magazine gives the weekend as "April 24–26", but
its own schedule has Outernet open Friday and Saturday and the Sunday at cheer
zones elsewhere; the venue says "the two days leading up to the London
Marathon". The record uses 24–25 April.

**The pin is the district.** Outernet is several buildings — the venue calls
this "the entire Outernet district" and pictures "The Now Building". The
coordinate is Outernet's, precision `approximate`.

**A design studio's credit is not used.** One studio's portfolio claims six
"immersive scenarios" for a Brooks London Marathon experience at Outernet, but
states no year; it is not in the record.

**The picture is the venue's own**, from its case study, with its alt text
"Brooks Running inside The Now Building".

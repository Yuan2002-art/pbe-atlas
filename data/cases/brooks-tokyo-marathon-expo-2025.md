## Description
**A stand in the room every entrant has to visit.** At the Tokyo Marathon EXPO
2025 — where runners collect their bibs — Brooks exhibited in a dedicated booth
at Tokyo Big Sight's South Halls, from Thursday 27 February to Saturday 1
March. It was run not by Brooks but by Achilles Corporation, which describes
itself as the brand's sole distributor in Japan.

**What was in it, in the distributor's words.** The latest shoes, led by the
HYPERION ELITE 4 PB racer, and apparel; "sales and experience content where
you could try the shoes" (販売及びシューズのトライアルができる体験コンテンツ)
inside the booth. "Each day many customers came, and it was a great success."

## Verification notes
**The brand did not speak; its distributor did.** The source is Achilles's own
report, filed as official-brand because Achilles acts for Brooks in Japan.
Nothing on a Brooks page was found for this booth.

**One of many stands.** The expo is the organiser's hall of exhibitors, as the
Ultra-Trail Village is at UTMB. This booth is written up because the
distributor documented it with photographs; the others at the expo are not
researched here. The organiser's own report could not be opened in this pass.

**No booth number or size** is given.

**The pin is Tokyo Big Sight**, not the booth; precision `approximate`.

**Photographs exist and are not yet stored.** The report carries four booth
photographs, waiting on approval to download.

## Description
**A podcast took a house and made it a studio with an audience in it.** From 24
to 31 August 2026 — eight days, a day longer than the race week itself — Dans
la Tête d'un Coureur ran the **DLTDC House** on Place du Triangle de l'Amitié,
"à quelques minutes seulement de l'épicentre de l'événement, la ligne de départ
et d'arrivée." It is operated by **Sunday Night Média**, the publication's
publisher, whose Directrice Générale is Lauriane Autissier.

**It is the first case in this Atlas whose occupant sells nothing.** Every other
record is a company with a product: shoes, watches, compression, beer, coffee
machines, a retailer's catalogue. This one has an audience and an episode
schedule, and the room exists to turn the first into the second.

**What happened in it.** The published programme is public podcast recordings,
conversations with athletes, artists and creators, practical workshops on
nutrition, care, recovery and movement, culinary experiences, yoga sessions and
"moments festifs."

**One episode is confirmed by the brand after the fact**, which is rare in this
dataset: an episode on what high-level trail running does to a couple, recorded
with Alix Noblat, Justine Carrel and Mathieu Masbernard — each the partner of a
professional trail runner rather than a runner. The publication's own page for
it closes: *"Un épisode enregistré à la DLTDC House, en collaboration avec
Shokz."*

**Shokz appears twice in this edition.** It is named here as a collaborator on
that episode, and it also holds the vinyl listening room inside i-Run's Home of
Mountains. A single audio brand placing itself inside two other parties' rooms
rather than taking one of its own.

## Verification notes
**The spine of this record is a press release, and it is labelled as one.** The
Presse Agence piece carries "rédigé à partir d'un communiqué de presse transmis
à la rédaction", so it is Sunday Night Média speaking through a title rather
than a title reporting on it. It is filed `other` for that reason, the same
treatment given to the sponsored trade feature on the Arc'teryx record.

**What lifts it above the press release is one line on the brand's own site.**
The episode page says the recording happened "à la DLTDC House" — first-party,
after the week, in the past tense. That is the only confirmation retrieved that
the house opened at all, and it confirms one session rather than the programme.

**Nothing else in the programme is confirmed.** The workshops, the yoga, the
culinary sessions and the festive moments are all announced and none is
reported. No attendance, no schedule and no second episode were found.

**Access is unknown.** Neither source says whether entry was free, ticketed or
by registration. The field is left empty rather than guessed.

**The address comes from the listing, not the brand.** Place du Triangle de
l'Amitié is the aggregator's; both first-party sources give only "a few minutes
from the start and finish line." The pin sits on the square and precision is
`approximate`.

**No images.** The only large picture served on the publication's pages is a
site-wide key visual that also appears on its 404 page, so it is the brand's
default artwork rather than anything showing this room. Nothing of the house —
inside or out — was retrieved, and a default graphic captioned as a space is
the kind of stand-in this Atlas does not carry.

**Partners are said to exist and are not named.** The release says the project
is "co-construit avec des partenaires"; only Shokz surfaced, and only through
one episode.

## Description
**A laboratory in a chalet.** The Enduraw Performance Center is a permanent
building at 330 Route du Plagnolet in Chamonix, run by Enduraw — a company that
describes itself as "a sport performance laboratory" doing "data science
applied to endurance performance."

**It is the first case in this Atlas where measurement is the mechanism.**
Enduraw's Testing product is "complete physiological testing protocols to
understand your body and optimize your training zones", and the centre's own
page names an *espace training et tests physiologiques*. The photograph of that
room shows what it actually is: a treadmill and a wheeled screen standing
between dry-stone piers under a timber ceiling. There is no clinical white
anywhere in the building.

**Three ways in, all sold.** The brand offers the centre as a **Training Camp**
— "a hyper-personalized 4 to 7-day sports immersion: testing, on-the-ground
coaching and full follow-up" — as a **Corporate Seminar** venue, "our spaces
available for your team meetings, in a unique setting facing the peaks", and as
**Coworking**, "a workspace facing Mont Blanc, to work in peace and quiet
between outings."

**What the rooms are.** Beyond the testing space, the published photographs
show a double-height living and recovery hall with a timber mezzanine and a
log-stack balustrade, a bar, and a relaxation and coworking floor under the
roof. A **Compex** stand — "Préparation, Récupération, Anti-douleur" — sits in
the recovery hall.

**Who it tests.** Enduraw's site lists Tom Evans, Ruth Croft, Petter Engdahl,
Nicolas Navarro, Meline Rollin and Duncan Perrillat under "Elite athletes". The
founder, Joseph Mestrallet, is described as having built pacing plans "from his
bedroom in Chamonix" before any of this existed.

## Verification notes
**The brand's own page contradicts the listing about where this building is.**
The aggregator that surfaced it places the Enduraw Performance Center in
**Argentière**, eight kilometres up the valley. Enduraw's own page gives the
address as **330 Route du Plagnolet, 74400 Chamonix**. The record follows the
brand. That address was not geocoded here, so the coordinate is Chamonix town
centre and precision is `city` — less precise than the address, deliberately.

**Nothing about UTMB week is recorded as fact.** The listing describes a
race-week programme at this centre — community trail runs on the Monday,
Tuesday and Thursday, a KOM challenge and a performance-and-data conference on
the Wednesday, each booked separately. None of it appears on Enduraw's own
pages and none of it is in the description above. There is also no
`relatedEvent`: this is a permanent building, and attaching it to an edition
because a third party listed four sessions there would turn a laboratory into
a race-week activation.

**No opening date is published**, so the date carries `precision: unknown` and
`ongoing: true`, the same shape as the Salomon store and Big Mountain Basecamp.

**Two of the four captions are the brand's words, not mine.** The testing space
and the recovery hall were looked at and described from the frame. The bar and
the coworking floor are captioned from Enduraw's own alt text, and say so on
the plate. It is a small thing, but a caption is a claim about what a reader is
looking at, and the two kinds should not be silently mixed.

**The photographs were resized, not otherwise altered.** Enduraw serves them at
around 7000px and 20-30MB each; they are stored here at 2000px wide. No crop,
no colour change.

**The Compex relationship is not established.** A branded stand in a photograph
is evidence that the equipment is there, not evidence of a partnership. It is
recorded as a collaborator with that role stated narrowly.

**What is not known:** what a test costs, what equipment beyond a treadmill the
protocols use, how long the centre has been open, and whether the elite
athletes listed are tested in this building or remotely.

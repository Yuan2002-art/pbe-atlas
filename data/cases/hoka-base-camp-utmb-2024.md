## Description
**The title partner's base, inside the organiser's Village.** For UTMB 2024 —
the first edition after the organiser announced, in April, that HOKA would hold
the title position through 2028 — HOKA ran its race week from "📍 HOKA Base Camp
- Ultra-Trail® Village".

**The programme, in HOKA's own post.** Tuesday 27 August: a shakeout run and
the opening of a "HOKA FLyLab Experience". Wednesday 28: sunrise yoga, the
launch of a "Tecton X 3 Chamonix signature edition", an athletes' meet and
greet, and in the evening a HOKA Film Festival by Montagne-en-Scène. Thursday
29: sunrise yoga again. Friday 30: a "HOKA Fly Zone" in the evening. "And
also... 👟 HOKA demo shoes for 24 hours, and many more surprises!"

**Where it moved by 2026.** In the 2026 edition HOKA's Base Camp was on the
terrace of La Folie Douce, outside the Village, with a FlyLab left on Place du
Mont-Blanc and a Refuge added at La Flégère — see that record. In 2024 the Base
Camp itself was in the Village.

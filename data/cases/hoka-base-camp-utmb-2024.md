## Description
**The title partner's base, inside the organiser's Village.** For UTMB 2024 —
the first edition after the organiser announced, in April, that HOKA would hold
the title position through 2028 — HOKA ran its race week from "📍 HOKA Base Camp
- Ultra-Trail® Village".

**The programme, in HOKA's own post.** Tuesday 27 August: a shakeout run and
the opening of a "HOKA FLyLab Experience". Wednesday 28: sunrise yoga, the
launch of a "Tecton X 3 Chamonix signature edition", an athletes' meet and
greet, and in the evening a HOKA Film Festival by Montagne-en-Scène. Thursday
29: sunrise yoga again. Friday 30: a "HOKA Fly Zone" in the evening. "And
also... 👟 HOKA demo shoes for 24 hours, and many more surprises!"

**Where it moved by 2026.** In the 2026 edition HOKA's Base Camp was on the
terrace of La Folie Douce, outside the Village, with a FlyLab left on Place du
Mont-Blanc and a Refuge added at La Flégère — see that record. In 2024 the Base
Camp itself was in the Village.

and "UTMB debut: 2022" through to the X3, and the darkened display room in use.

checked by comparing them pixel by pixel against the files on disk rather than
by eye.

**The capture dates could not be re-read.** The file names carry them -- 25,
27, 28 and 29 August -- and the portfolio prefixes them "UTMB-2024-BRAND", but
the host serves resized copies with the metadata stripped, so the dates rest on
the file names and the project page rather than on the files themselves.

**The 25 August frame predates the opening**, and is captioned as the build
rather than as the week.

**Read from a Facebook post, once.** The post's text was read from its page
metadata in a single visit; no other Facebook content was collected. HOKA's
own registration page linked from the post was not read.

**The year is established by the weekdays** — Tuesday 27 August falls in 2024
— and by the Tecton X 3 launch, which the blog dates to that week in its post
of 21 August 2024. The post itself carries no date.

**What the FLY LAB was, per its builder:** "a state-of-the-art retail suite
where runners could discover the innovation, engineering and technology of the
latest running footwear", where "Athletes were invited to demo footwear". A
panel photographed inside it reads "A demo pair is waiting for you at HOKA
Basecamp". The builder's page carries no date; its images are in a September
2024 folder and it names the Tecton X3, which launched that week.

**When the partnership began is given two ways.** The organiser's 2024
announcement says HOKA "began its partnership with UTMB World Series in
2022"; the 2026 record gives October 2021 for its entry as technical partner.
Not settled here.

**The pin is the Village**, precision `approximate`; no stand position was
published.

**Pictures from beyond the cited sources.** The pictures were found in later
coverage — portfolios, newsletters, the press — and each was looked at before
captioning. Where a caption places a picture by inference rather than by the

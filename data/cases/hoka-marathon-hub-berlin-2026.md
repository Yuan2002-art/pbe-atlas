## Description
**A radio station, moved into a brand's hub for a week.** For the 2026 Berlin
Marathon HOKA opens a *Marathon Hub* at Leipziger Straße 60, and Berlin's
community station Refuge Worldwide moves "our radio operations from
Niemetzstrasse" into it from Tuesday 22 to Sunday 27 September. The station
broadcasts "from 10AM daily as usual, with a special HOKA show each day at
2PM" on running, wellness and nutrition.

**Around the broadcast.** The hub is "open to come by and hangout", with talks
and shake-out runs, "a video installation, coffee from Cafe Tiergarten, product
personalisation, cheer sign making and more". Parties open and close the week —
Lakuti on the Tuesday, Palms Trax, Courtney Bailey and SALIMATA on the Sunday
night — and the Refuge run crew runs from there on the Saturday morning.

**On race day it faces the course.** "We will be moving the music outside to
create a cheer zone for the runners going by… Come join us at kilometre 40!"

**Hours:** opening party from 19:00 on 22 September; 11:00–21:00 from 23 to 26
September; 08:00–16:00 on race day, then an afterparty from 20:00.

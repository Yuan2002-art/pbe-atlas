## Description
**A café handed over for a week.** For Western States 2025, Coffeebar in
Olympic Valley — the race's start — "handed over the keys (and the coffee) to
our friends at @hoka for a full transformation of our café—wall-to-wall
branding, bold visuals, and trail running energy everywhere you look." From 23
to 27 June it was "the official HOKA Outpost, a hub for WSER runners, fans,
and storytellers alike", open daily 7:00 to 17:00 and still serving its own
menu.

**A studio in the back room.** "Freetrail podcast recordings with @dylanbo
kick off today from our fully converted back room studio." The organiser's
agenda lists the same show, "Trailgating", at "the HOKA Outpost at Coffeebar":
"Freetrail's live show packed with athlete interviews, sharp race insights,
and unbeatable trail vibes". HOKA's own media teams worked from there,
"capturing the energy of the village".

**The start of the official shakeout.** On Thursday 26 June the official WSER
shakeout run left "right outside the café" at 7:30.

**A built set, not a dressed café.** The studio that made it, Rhoam, describes
the brief as "a studio set for HOKA's media partners" and the build as "the
back room of a coffee shop in Olympic Valley transformed into an immersive set
in less than 24 hours." Its stated approach was "less cowboy cliche, more
living archive. Using vintage maps, tin cans, co-op flour sacks and ramen
boxes". What that produced is a fictional general store whose entire stock is
printed with names from the course: "Foresthill" ramen, "Foresthill Flour
Co-op" sacks marked "located at mile 62", a "Michigan Bluff first aid
all-inclusive kit", "Rucky Chucky chunk light tuna", "Green Gate solar
powered", "Last Chance propane", "Lyon Ridge tarp", "Ford's Bar" safety
matches, and pennants for Auburn and Placer — the town and the school where the
race finishes. The wall behind the presenters carries a timber "Freetrail ×"
sign.

## Verification notes
**The same set appears on HOKA's 2026 page, and that raises a question this
record cannot settle.** HOKA's hub for Western States 2026 illustrates its
"Freetrail WSER Live Coverage" listing with a photograph of what is
unmistakably this set -- the same "Freetrail x" sign, the same fictional stock,
the same "Rod Farvard" pennant. Either the set was put up again in 2026, or the
2026 page is illustrated with a 2025 photograph. **The second is entirely
possible**: the same page uses a photograph filenamed `HOKA_2022_WSER` for
another listing. The image is stored here because the set is this record's
subject, and its caption refuses the date rather than guessing it. If the set
did run again in 2026, that is a second case and not this one.

**The venue speaks, not the brand.** The main source is the café's own post,
filed as `other`. No HOKA page for the Outpost was read. The organiser's
archived agenda independently confirms the name and the Freetrail show.

**Read from Instagram once.** The post's text was read from its page metadata
in a single visit.

**The café returned in 2026.** The organiser's 2026 agenda starts the official
shakeout at "HOKA Ground Support in Coffeebar"; that is described in the 2026
FlyLab Trail record rather than as a record of its own, because no source
describes the café's 2026 fit-out.

**The pin is the café,** from its map entry; precision `exact`.

**Ten pictures, all from the studio that built the set.** Rhoam's project page
is the only retrieved source with images of the Outpost, and it is a primary
one: the studio designed, styled and built the room, and credits its own
photographers, Aisha McAdams and Ryan Thrower. Two of the ten are **frames from
the live broadcast, not photographs** — they carry the show's on-screen marks
and were captioned as such. Six of the remaining eight are the studio's own
two-up plates, two frames laid side by side; the captions say so rather than
describing a pair as one picture.

**The images were served on a fixed black canvas**, the portfolio's page
background baked in around each plate. That padding was cropped off before
storing, and nothing else was altered. Anything over 2000px was resized.

**The people in the portraits are not named** on the source page, so they are
not named here. The "Rod Farvard" pennant is set dressing and is not evidence
that the runner it names appears in any frame.

**"29 June" is printed on the ramen boxes** and is quoted, not interpreted. The
event record gives the race start as Saturday 28 June 2025, finishing at Placer
High School in Auburn.

**The studio's page carries no month or day**, only "2025". The year is
corroborated by the brief's own words, "2025 Western States preview", and by
the café's dates in the main source.

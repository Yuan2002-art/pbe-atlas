## Description
**A café handed over for a week.** For Western States 2025, Coffeebar in
Olympic Valley — the race's start — "handed over the keys (and the coffee) to
our friends at @hoka for a full transformation of our café—wall-to-wall
branding, bold visuals, and trail running energy everywhere you look." From 23
to 27 June it was "the official HOKA Outpost, a hub for WSER runners, fans,
and storytellers alike", open daily 7:00 to 17:00 and still serving its own
menu.

**A studio in the back room.** "Freetrail podcast recordings with @dylanbo
kick off today from our fully converted back room studio." The organiser's
agenda lists the same show, "Trailgating", at "the HOKA Outpost at Coffeebar":
"Freetrail's live show packed with athlete interviews, sharp race insights,
and unbeatable trail vibes". HOKA's own media teams worked from there,
"capturing the energy of the village".

**The start of the official shakeout.** On Thursday 26 June the official WSER
shakeout run left "right outside the café" at 7:30.

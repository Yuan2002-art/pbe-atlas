## Description
**A retailer's chalet, given to a different brand each day.** For UTMB
Mont-Blanc 2026 i-Run opened The Home of Mountains at 73 Chemin du Ce in
Chamonix — in its own words a pop-up, and in U Run's words "son chalet
spécifiquement ouvert pour l'occasion toute cette semaine". It is the first case
in this Atlas whose occupant is not arguing for its own products.

The room was built as four standing spaces, each carrying a partner's name:
**L'atelier café by La Marzocco**, running espresso basics, tastings and
extraction technique; **L'atelier workshop by Le Papoteur**, with airbrush
customisation, sewing and keyring making; **L'espace recovery by Reboots**, with
cold baths, pressotherapy boots, massage guns and Recovery Seats; and **L'espace
musique by Kokonut Bounce & Shokz**, a vinyl listening room set up "comme un
salon cosy" around "un rituel d'écoute plus attentif et immersif".

Across those spaces ran a day-by-day programme, and each day belonged to a
different brand: a coffee and meet on Monday 24 August with Théo Detienne and
Pierre Galbourdin; runs and bodypainting on the Tuesday with the ASICS athletes
Dorian Louvet, Marie Goncalves, Diane Rassineux and Sylvaine Cussot, alongside
Hemtonkiné; a Salomon S/LAB Genesis 2 shoe test on the Wednesday with Salomon
product manager Roger Espinet; a customisation workshop on the Thursday with
François d'Haene and Le Papoteur; a Garmin Fenix 9 session with Garmin's own
teams; and on Friday 28 August a "journée 100 % récupération & bien-être" with
HOKA, built from coffee, massage, yoga and an upcycling workshop.

The concept is credited by i-Run to itself and **Fraich'TOUCH**, and illustrated
by the Toulouse artist **Supraw**, whose character **Will** — "randonneur,
grimpeur, trailer, contemplatif" — carries the visual identity. i-Run gives the
space's register as art, music, gastronomy, cinema and outdoor culture, a place
where "visiteurs, artistes, athlètes et marques se rencontrent".

**Entry was not open.** i-Run's own page marks the chalet "ÉVÉNEMENT PRIVÉ -
I-RUN CLUB INSCRIPTION OBLIGATOIRE" — a private event with compulsory
registration through the i-Run Club.

## Verification notes
**The listing and the brand contradict each other about access, and the brand
wins.** The third-party listing that surfaced this case calls the chalet "Free,
with no registration for the chalet". i-Run's own page marks it a private event
with compulsory i-Run Club registration, and U Run tells readers places are
limited and to sign up through the club. Two published sources agree against the
listing, so the record follows them. The discrepancy is kept here rather than
silently resolved.

**This is not the La Marzocco case the lead described.** The listing frames the
space as "La Marzocco x i-Run", which reads as a two-brand collaboration. On
i-Run's own page La Marzocco holds one of four named corners and is not in the
space's name, and U Run's article does not mention La Marzocco at all. The case
is therefore filed under i-Run, with La Marzocco as a collaborator. **No La
Marzocco brand record has been created**, because on this evidence La Marzocco is
a tenant in someone else's room rather than a subject of its own.

**`primarySpatialType` is `pop-up` and there is no secondary.** i-Run's own page
title calls it "Pop-up à Chamonix", which settles the primary. It was checked
against `activation` — a guest in premises the brand does not own — and the
distinction did not survive: this is one chalet at one address, leased and
programmed by i-Run itself, not a stand inside the organiser's village. **It was
expected to be a hybrid and it is not.** One space, one form.

**The dates conflict, and the brand publishes none.** The listing gives 22–30
August. U Run gives the race week as "Du 24 au 30 août 2026" and says the chalet
was open "toute la semaine", and the sessions it lists run Monday 24 to Friday
28. i-Run's own page carries no dates at all. The record follows the published
week, 24–30 August; **the 22nd rests on the listing alone** and those two extra
days are not asserted here.

**Opening hours are not recorded.** The listing gives a True Artisan Café daily
09:00–15:00 with guest roasters and a barista workshop at 11:00 with fifteen
places. None of that appears on i-Run's page or in U Run, and "True Artisan Café"
is a La Marzocco programme name that appears in the listing only. It is left out
of the facts layer.

**The address is sourced, but indirectly.** 73 Chemin du Ce, 74400
Chamonix-Mont-Blanc is the destination of i-Run's own "Voir l'itinéraire" link
rather than text on the page. The listing said the address had not been published
and put its pin on Chamonix centre; this pin is the brand's own map link and it
is a street number, so precision is recorded as `exact`.

**Nothing after the fact.** U Run was published on 19 August 2026, before the
week, and i-Run's page is written in the present tense of a live event. There is
no post-event coverage and no attendance figure. The brand's page now carries
an undated gallery, in a folder named for 2026, showing the rooms in use; it
does not say when the photographs were taken, so nothing here confirms how the
space was actually used during the week. This is intent and plan, not
observation.

**A 2025 predecessor exists, and it is a different record.** i-Run ran **The
House of Mountains** at UTMB 2025 — a different name, in the L'M brasserie, open
"depuis le début de l'été", with the valley roaster **Shouka** on coffee rather
than La Marzocco, SportOrthese rather than Reboots on recovery, and the Club des
Sports de Chamonix as a local partner. Its page is cited here as context only.
Per rule 3 that is a separate edition and would be a separate case; the Atlas has
no UTMB Mont-Blanc 2025 event record, so it has not been written.

**Not recorded, though reported elsewhere:** a stated 550 m² floor area, a
three-month June-to-September run, an "i-Run Lab" multi-brand shoe-testing space
and a figure of fifty thousand visitors all appear in search summaries and in a
stand-builder's and an awards entry's material. Every one of them attaches to the
**2025** House, not to this chalet, and none was read on a page that states it
about 2026.

## Description
**A print counter in the city's main shopping street.** From Wednesday 26 to
Sunday 30 August 2026, lululemon ran a *Print Shop* at Pitt Street Mall, on the
corner of Market Street: "Purchase your new lululemon race day kit and
customise it with exclusive Sydney 2026 designs, printed while you wait."

**How it worked, in the brand's words.** Printing was "available on select
styles purchased from our CBD stores", new and with tags on. Visitors chose from
"limited-edition Print Shop graphics", up to two per garment, then either left
it and were texted when it was ready, or could "Queue up and watch your product
get customised in front of you". No booking, first come first served; nothing
shipped; no returns once printed.

**Its twin.** The Record Shop in Darlinghurst, from the same microsite, sold
nothing at all. That is a separate record.

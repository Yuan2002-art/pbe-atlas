## Description
**A record shop that sells nothing.** For 2026 Sydney Marathon weekend,
Friday 28 to Sunday 30 August, lululemon opened a *Record Shop* at 121 Riley
Street, Darlinghurst — "The home of music, mindset and your running mantra this
race week." The brand's own FAQ is plain about it: "Can I purchase product at
the Record Shop? No. This is a community hub."

**What you could do there.** "Sydney's finest DJs spin on the lululemon
soundsystem. Drop in, lay down your mantra, customise a record sleeve, or chill
with your crew over coffee." Open to all, free, outside the programmed
sessions.

**Four programmed moments.** Friday: *Set your record*, "a 60-minute hosted
session exploring how music and mindset can elevate your race", with Derrick
Gee on music and Ryan Mannix on mindset. Saturday: two shakeouts hosted by
Katie Martin — the second "Added by popular demand" — each back for a set by
Cosmo's Midnight. Sunday: The Afterparty with Tempo Journal, Harry Hayes on the
decks.

**Its twin.** The same microsite ran a Print Shop at Pitt Street Mall, which
did sell. That is a separate record.

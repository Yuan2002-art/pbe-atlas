## Description
**A gallery on the marathon route, turned over to the city's crews.** For the
2026 TCS London Marathon weekend, New Balance — the race's official apparel and
footwear partner — took Somerset House's Embankment Galleries, "on the bank of
the River Thames, along the marathon route", and opened *London Run House*
from Friday 24 to Sunday 26 April.

**A film and a set of portraits at the centre.** "At the heart of London Run
House is a cinematic documentary and portrait series created in collaboration
with London's running communities, capturing how the city prepares for
marathon day across its boroughs." The portraits, by photographer Vivek
Vadoliya, show members of Run The Boroughs, Runner Beans, Deaf Run Club, LES
Run and 2-Step Collective. The film premiered in the space on the Friday.

**Around it, nine amenities.** A bar and lounge; compression boots and
massage guns; the film and portrait exhibition; medal engraving; heat-press
T-shirt printing "with London-focused designs"; "Test & Trial treadmill
stations featuring the latest New Balance running footwear"; a product
showcase; British Sign Language education; and race-day nails and hair.

**Three days, each with a job.** Friday was preparation — a pre-race body
regulation session and a Runner Beans arts-and-crafts workshop. Saturday
carried a LES Run collage workshop, a women's pre-race relaxation lounge with
LES Run and PASSA, and a 5 km sunset run to test the new Ellipse. Sunday split
into a spectator party in the morning and a finisher party with medal
engraving and posters in the afternoon, while crews ran cheer zones at Mile 8,
16.3 and 25.

**Public hours**, per Retail Gazette: Friday 14:00–17:00, Saturday
10:00–18:00, Sunday 09:00–17:00.

## Description
**Four days at a hired studio.** For the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon,
New Balance opened Run House at Ideal Glass Studios, 9 West 8th Street, from
30 October to 2 November 2026. EventMarketer (6 November 2026) and WWD
Footwear News both give those dates. The address, including the postcode
10011, is WWD's.

**What both articles put in the room.** Customisation, shoe try-on, massage,
and a recovery fuel bar.

**Two different credits, both editorial.** EventMarketer calls New Balance
the Marathon Expo's presenting sponsor. WWD calls New Balance an official
partner. Neither article is the organiser, and the brand's own newsroom
returned 403, so neither credit is an organiser confirmation. The studio is
not the Expo. The presenting-sponsor line is about the Expo, not a
description of this room.

**After the race.** EventMarketer describes the room turning into a
celebration point, with limited apparel sold there. The name of that apparel
is not stated here.

## Description
**A hanok in Bukchon, opened 30 October 2026.** Nike ACG opened ACG Bukchon
Maru — Financial News prints the name as "ACG 북촌 마루(ACG Bukchon Maru)" —
in a traditional hanok in Bukchon, Jongno-gu, Seoul. The brand's Korea ACG
page gives the opening as "2026년 10월 30일부터". Yonhap describes the opening
as "30일 개점…약 6개월간 운영", and Herald Business calls it a "Six-month
pop-up". No page states an end date.

**Five zones.** Yonhap and Financial News describe five areas: Listening
Trail, Community Circle, an Expedition Room with a Gear Up Table, a Courtyard,
and a Hanok Lab.

**A programme, not a single day.** The brand page lists an ACG kimchi-making
hike (ACG 김장 하이킹), a winter-solstice night hike marked Coming Soon (동지
야간 하이킹), and a Q&A. Yonhap describes running and hiking in the Bukchon
area, programming tied to the seasons and to tradition, and a stamp route
made with local shops. The partners are not named.

**What is named for sale or customisation.** The brand page names the ACG
Lava series. Yonhap names ACG Ultrafly, ACG Zegama Hike, and goods exclusive
to Bukchon. Financial News describes ACG By You customisation, and separately
recounts the development of a trail shoe it calls 피클주스 (Pickle Juice).

**Who spoke at the press event.** Financial News reports Jack Oshima, Sean
Schmidt, and ACG trail runner 고민철. The English forms of the two directors'
roles — Asia brand director, and Asia business director — are a translation
and have to be re-read on the page. The English spelling of 고민철 is not
confirmed.

**The brand's stated reason for the site**, as Yonhap reports it: Bukchon
keeps traditional architecture and local culture, and it connects to Bukaksan.

## Verification notes
**No end date is recorded, and none was calculated.** Yonhap and Herald
Business say about six months. That phrase was not turned into a closing day.
The date field therefore has a start and no end. On the page that formats as
the opening day alone. That is a limit of the date fields, not a claim that
the space lasted one day. `ongoing` is left false: that flag is for permanent
retail that is still open, and this space is described as temporary.

**`pop-up` asks for a defined close.** The vocabulary wants an open and a
close. Only the open is a date. The Herald's "Six-month pop-up" is why the
form is still `pop-up` rather than permanent retail. The gap is also in the
activation-logic rationale.

**The street address is not adopted.** The brand page does not give one.
"서울 종로구 북촌로5길 14" appears only in search snippets, listed below, and
is not the pin. The pin is Nominatim's node for the name 북촌한옥마을
(Bukchon Hanok Village), node 2669684119, at 126.9858648, 37.5823919, on
Bukchon-ro 12-gil. It is a named place inside the district Yonhap describes,
not a surveyed door. Precision is `approximate`. The snippet address was not
sent to the geocoder.

**Hours are not recorded.** 11:00–20:00 appears only in a snippet.

**Local partners and the designer are unknown.** Financial News refers to
partners in Bukchon and gives no list. No opened page names an architect,

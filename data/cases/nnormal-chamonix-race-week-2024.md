## Description
**Four sessions, four rooms, none of them the brand's.** In UTMB week 2024,
Monday 26 to Thursday 29 August, NNormal ran one session a day in Chamonix,
each in a different venue: Le Petit Social, two hotels and a microbrewery. "This
year, we are back in Chamonix with a week full of activities."

**The four sessions, by day:**

- **Mon 26 Aug, 9:30 · Le Petit Social** — a community run with the Kilian
  Jornet Foundation and CREA Mont-Blanc, with an expert guide, "to learn more
  about the Chamonix valley and its biodiversity", testing Kjerag and Tomir 2.0,
  then coffee.
- **Tue 27 Aug, 17:00 · Hotel Le Prieuré** — a screening of the brand's film
  *Into the (Un)known*, "a journey through Kilian Jornet's mind during one of
  the toughest challenges of his career", and a Q&A with the NNormal team.
- **Wed 28 Aug, 9:30 · Hotel Les Cosmiques** — a shoe test and community run
  with Find Your Everest "on the trails of the Mont-Blanc massif".
- **Thu 29 Aug, 15:00 · MBC Chamonix Microbrewery** — a session on Kilian's
  Alpine Connections, "connecting the 4000m peaks of the Alps", with the KJF
  and four experts: mountain guide and skier Vivian Bruchez, glaciologist
  Christophe Ogier, NNormal's Mireia Miró and trail runner Hillary Gerardi.

**The co-founder was elsewhere.** A local blog noted before the week that "Kilian
will be notably absent from UTMB this year and is spending his time instead
scrambling across 4,000M Peaks in the Alps", while "his NNormal team will be on
sight selling shoes and sharing the story of his adventures". The Thursday
session was about exactly that.

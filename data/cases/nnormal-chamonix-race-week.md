## Description
**A race week with no room of its own.** Between 24 and 28 August 2026
NNormal ran eight sessions in Chamonix, in five venues, none of them its own:
a café, a microbrewery, a guinguette, a hotel, and a scientific
research centre. It had no shop, stand, chalet or house for the week. The
brand's own event listings are the whole record: every session was booked
through NNormal's Eventbrite page, free, with a waitlist on most.

**The eight sessions, by day:**

- **Mon 24 Aug, 15:00–17:00 · La Guinguette**, 55 Chemin des Gourgnes —
  *Women's Cadí shoe test & talks* with Emelie, Joyce and Lucille: "Run and
  test the new NNormal shoe Cadí and join a Q&A on product, resilience and
  community."
- **Tue 25 Aug, 09:00–11:30 · ARCUS Coffee**, 263 Avenue Cachat le Géant —
  *NNormal shoes & Julbo sunglasses test with Lucille Germain*, then
  breakfast.
- **Tue 25 Aug, 15:00–17:00 · MBC Chamonix Microbrewery**, 350 Route du
  Bouchet — *NNormal Films & Conversations*: "three NNormal films exploring
  creativity, resilience and purpose, followed by conversations and an
  apéro."
- **Wed 26 Aug, 08:30–13:30 · CREA Mont-Blanc research centre**, 67 Lacets du
  Belvédère — *Running Minds and Cadí GORE-TEX® test*, "A NNormal x GORE-TEX®
  Brand event, in collaboration with the Kilian Jornet Foundation."
- **Wed 26 Aug, 15:30–17:30 · Heliopic Hotel & Spa**, 610 Route Blanche —
  *NNormal gear test & apéro*.
- **Thu 27 Aug, 10:00–12:30 · CREA Mont-Blanc research centre** — *A breakfast
  for those who want to explore trail running differently*: "Discover
  Mont-Blanc's alpine ecosystems with CREA Mont-Blanc and the Kilian Jornet
  Foundation."
- **Thu 27 Aug, 15:00–17:00 · MBC Chamonix Microbrewery** — *Trail Keepers:
  film premiere and roundtable*, "exploring people, mountains and trails".
- **Fri 28 Aug, 09:00–11:30 · ARCUS Coffee** — *NNormal gear test & light
  breakfast*.

**Five are product tests, three are not.** The tests put the Cadí — and a
Cadí GORE-TEX version — on runners' feet. The other three are two film
afternoons and a breakfast about alpine ecosystems, with no product in their
descriptions.

**It is how the brand works everywhere.** The same organiser page lists
NNormal's summer as a run of free community runs and shoe tests at other
people's premises — an outdoor retailer in Grenoble, a women's run club in Brussels,
a civic centre in the Cerdanya, a town square in Garmisch — and Chamonix
followed the pattern at a larger scale.

## Verification notes
**First filed as not a case.** This was recorded in `research/README.md` as a
lead closed without a case, because the Atlas records spaces and NNormal had
none. The author judged the absence itself worth recording, and a new spatial
type, `distributed`, was added for it; this is its first case. The earlier
note also said the Foundation presented three sessions as its own — true of
the Foundation's page, but all eight sessions, those three included, are
organised under NNormal's own name on Eventbrite.

**A 2024 precedent exists.** NNormal's 2024 week was also four sessions in four
borrowed venues, one of them the same microbrewery; it is its own record, so
the two editions can be compared.

**The pin is the town, not a venue.** No single venue is the space, so the
coordinate is Chamonix centre and precision is `city`. The five addresses are
in the description, each from the event's own page.

**"UTMB" does not appear in any of the sources.** The sessions fall on the
race week's dates, and the Foundation's page speaks of "a week" in which
"Chamonix becomes a global meeting point for the trail running community".
The record links the edition on the dates, as the Goldwin installation does.

**Written from the plan.** The pages are booking pages; they now read "Sales
Ended". Nothing retrieved says how many came.

**Names are as the brand gives them.** "Emelie, Joyce & Lucille" are first
names in the event title. A third-party listing gives them as Emelie Forsberg,
Joyce Njeru and Lucille Germain; only Lucille Germain is named in full by
NNormal, on the 25 August page, and the other two surnames are not recorded.

**Not counted:** a third-party listing names a nutrition partner for the
women's test and sets the Films afternoon's three titles and film-makers;
neither is on the pages read here. The Trail Keepers film's producer is not
recorded either — the event page does not say.

**Eight pictures, and they are the evidence for this case's own shape.** Each
session card is a NNormal graphic with the session title, day, time and
**venue printed on it** -- La Guinguette, Arcus Coffee, MBC Chamonix, Heliopic
Hotel, CREA Mont-Blanc. Not one of them shows a venue; the photographs behind
the type are runners and woodland. So they document the `distributed` reading

almost every session, and never its own.

**The brand's own two sources disagree about one session's time.** The card

## Strategic purpose
Be present at the sport's biggest week without building anything. NNormal puts
its shoe on runners' feet and its name on other people's doors, and spends the
budget on programme rather than on structure. In a week when the brands around
it built chalets, stands and houses, this one booked rooms.

## Archive use
The register's counter-example, and the reason `distributed` exists as a form.
Every other case here is a brand that made a space; this is a brand that
declined to, at the same event, in the same week. It is what makes the chalets
legible as a choice rather than as the only way to be present.

## Why this location?
Five rooms, none of them a retail environment, and the range is the argument. A
guinguette, a café, a microbrewery, a hotel and a high-altitude ecology research
centre have nothing in common except that they are places Chamonix already uses.
The brand reaches its audience inside the town's own institutions rather than
pulling it into a built one — and the research centre in particular buys a kind
of seriousness no fit-out could manufacture.

## Why this timing?
Monday to Friday, one or two sessions a day, finishing before the race starts.
The week is timed to the runner's preparation rather than to the spectacle:
everything happens while people still have legs and attention, and nothing
competes with the event itself. A brand with a stand needs the crowd; a brand
with a booking page needs the calendar.

## Why this event?
The audience is already assembled and already predisposed. UTMB week brings the
brand's exact market into one valley for seven days, which is precisely the
condition under which not building becomes viable — the reach a chalet buys is
already there in the street. NNormal holds no partnership with the organiser and
needs none, because nothing it does requires the organiser's ground.

## Why this product?
The Cadí is new, and a new shoe has to be run in rather than looked at. Five of
the eight sessions end with the shoe on someone's feet on Chamonix terrain,
including a GORE-TEX version tested out of a research station. The product needs
trials, not a plinth, and trials need a meeting point rather than a shop.

## What performance claim is being reinforced?
That the shoe is tested where it will be used, by people who will use it. The
claim rests on the trial rather than on the setting — there is no built
environment here doing any persuading, which is itself the position: a brand
confident enough in the product to let a borrowed café be the whole apparatus.

## What cultural meaning is being built?
Restraint as a signature. Three of the eight sessions carry no product at all —
two film afternoons and a breakfast about alpine ecosystems with a research
centre and the Kilian Jornet Foundation — and their presence says the brand is
interested in the mountain rather than only in the market for it. The borrowed
venues do the same work: a brand that arrives as a guest reads as part of the
place, where a brand that builds reads as visiting it.

## What role does the physical experience play?
Essential and deliberately unowned. The experience cannot be removed — a shoe
test is physical or it is nothing — but the *space* can be, and is. What the
brand controls is the session; what it borrows is the room. That division is the
whole spatial strategy, and it is why this record's coordinate is a town rather
than an address.

## Experience mechanism
Invitation. Every session is a booking with a finite number of places, free, most
with a waitlist, and the audience is self-selected at the point of registering
rather than captured by walking past. Where HOKA's week envelops a visitor who
never chose to enter, this one only reaches people who put their name down — a
smaller audience, more deeply engaged, and legible to the brand by name.

## Key strategic insight
A brand can occupy an event without occupying any ground. By spending on
programme instead of structure, NNormal converts other people's premises into a
distributed venue that exists only in its own calendar — and the absence of a
built space stops reading as a missing budget and starts reading as a position.
The proof that it is a position rather than a constraint is that it happened
twice: the 2024 week was the same shape, in the same town, in some of the same
rooms.

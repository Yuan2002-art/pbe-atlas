## Description
**A factory robot on Newbury Street, a year before London.** From 17 to 22
April 2025, 11:00 to 18:00, On ran *On Labs Boston* at 400 Newbury Street —
"a space where you can see our latest innovations in action."

**The robot, then the treadmill.** "Got three minutes? Watch a robot spray our
lightest upper live. Then, hop on a treadmill and feel the future." The daily
programme, "Experience LightSpray™ Live", ran through opening hours: "Watch the
robot in action, as it sprays new uppers. And feel the future when you test run
the Cloudboom Strike LS on one of our treadmills."

**And the shoe, in limited numbers.** Visitors could "own the Cloudboom Strike
LS, seen on podiums worldwide", "Exclusively available in limited quantities".
Around it: a "Marathon Edit" of race gear, a free race-day kit, a live course
preview, and free flowers and tattoos.

## Verification notes

resolves; the Wayback capture is from 27 March 2025, three weeks ahead. The
year is established by the dates (17–22 April falls on race week 2025) and the
capture date; the page's own footer reads "© On 2024".

**The robot's trajectory across three records.** The robot is documented in
Boston in 2025 (this record) and in London in 2026; On's Boston page for 2026
does not mention it. Whether it was in Boston in 2026 is not known.

**The same address, another brand, a year later.** A retailer's 2026 guide
places Brooks's Hyperion House at 400 Newbury Street. Nothing retrieved says
how the unit is let.

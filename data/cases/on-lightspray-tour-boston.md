## Description
**The same tour, a week before London.** From 16 to 21 April 2026, 11:00 to
18:00, On ran the *On LightSpray™ Tour Boston* at 660 Beacon Street — "the next chapter of running is sprayed."

**What the brand says was in it.** Visitors could "step into a space where
dreams take shape, innovation comes to life up close", then "Try on the
LightSpray Cloudmonster 3 Hyper and LightSpray Cloudboom Strike" — both
"Available now" there. The page promises a "Dream Run", as in London, without
describing it.

## Verification notes
**Read from an archive.** The live page returns 403; the record uses the
Wayback Machine's capture of 30 April 2026. Only the address, dates, hours and
the lines quoted above are used.

**No robot is documented in Boston.** London's sources put the spraying robot
at the centre of that room. Boston's page speaks only of experiencing the
technology "up close". Whether the robot travelled is not stated, so this
record does not assume it.

**A permanent store is reported, not verified.** A research pass found a trade
report that On opened its first Boston store on Newbury Street the same week.
It was not read in this pass, and a store would be a different record.

## Description
**A factory robot, brought to a square in Mayfair for marathon weekend.** From
24 to 26 April 2026 On staged the UK debut of its LightSpray technology at 3
Hanover Square: *On LightSpray Tour London*, "an immersive world co-created by
PlayLab".

**The robot is the exhibit.** "Runners will watch the LightSpray™ robot in
action". On describes the process as one in which "a robotic arm sprays 1.5
kilometers of specialized filament onto a last, creating an ultra-light,
one-piece upper in around three minutes", condensing "approximately 200 steps
across multiple factories". The robot ran from 12:00 to 19:00 on the Friday and
11:00 to 19:00 on Saturday and Sunday.

**Then the test run, and a voice.** After the robot, visitors stepped "into a
data-driven product test run dubbed the 'Dream Run'". Audio clips of Hellen
Obiri — who would race London that Sunday — played "throughout the space",
recounting her runs in the shoe.

**Around it, a programme with Healf.** Movement sessions and workshops on the
Friday and Saturday, a pre-marathon party on the Friday evening, and a recovery
zone on the Sunday, "hosted in collaboration with Healf, a UK-based wellbeing
platform".

**The shoes.** The LightSpray Cloudboom Strike, "On's lightest-ever racing
shoe at 170g", and the LightSpray Cloudmonster 3 Hyper, "the first shoe upper
sprayed at the Korean facility".

Performance Testing was not assigned.

**No sale is documented.** Wallpaper* notes the shoes "are available in
limited-edition drops" in general; neither source says they were sold at
Hanover Square.

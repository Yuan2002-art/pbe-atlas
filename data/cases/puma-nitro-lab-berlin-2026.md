## Description
**PUMA's third NITRO Lab in this register.** For the 2026 Berlin Marathon PUMA
announces "the return of its iconic NITRO™ Lab", "Located at the 39km mark on
the marathon course", opening on Thursday 24 September at 10:00.

**What is announced for it.** "Challenges, customisation and product
giveaways", and the return of the 1km Treadmill Challenge: "win an exclusive
pair of Deviate NITRO™ Elite 4, so long as you can run 1km in under 4
minutes." Items from an exclusive collaboration with Another Cotton Lab — the
German label behind Sunday Running Club — are among the giveaways.

**A relay that ends at the door.** "The build to Berlin begins on September
22, as four teams of seven runners will compete in the PUMA418 Race. This
418km relay race goes from the PUMA HQ in Herzogenaurach to The NITRO™ Lab in
Berlin." On Saturday 26 September PUMA and Another Cotton Lab host a "Run &
Rave" 5 km shakeout with a local DJ.

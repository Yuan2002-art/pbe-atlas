## Description
**A room that got brighter the harder you ran.** For the 2026 London Marathon
weekend PUMA took over the Institute of Contemporary Arts on The Mall — the
finish straight — for "the pop-up PUMA NITRO LAB". PUMA's own summary: it
would "host product customisation and shakeout runs", with free pizza and beer
for finishers on the Sunday.

**Three zones, per the AV builder.** "Live trainer customisation, a
high-energy 1K treadmill challenge, and an innovation hall showcasing PUMA's
latest NITRO™ technology."

**The treadmill zone was the instrument.** Above it hung a ceiling of
transparent LED panels; at the centre of the space stood a 6m x 3m LED wall.
"Each treadmill wirelessly broadcast its health monitoring data into
TouchDesigner, where the team translated live biometric output into a
generative visual layer", composited with live camera feeds and mapped across
the wall and ceiling. In iMAG's words: "The room responded to the people in
it. The harder participants pushed, the more the environment changed around
them."

**Hours**, per trade press: Friday 24 April 12:00–19:00, Saturday 10:00–18:00,
and Sunday 13:00–15:00 for finishers.

**Where it sat.** The ICA is on The Mall, where the London Marathon finishes.
PUMA's flagship on Oxford Street did medal engraving on the Sunday and Monday,
as a separate space.

## Verification notes
**Three days or four.** iMAG calls it "the four-day activation"; trade and
editorial listings give Friday to Sunday, 24–26 April. The record uses the
three published public days; the fourth may be a build, press or invite day.

**iMAG's page carries no year.** It is tied to 2026 by PUMA's own April 2026
page naming the ICA pop-up, and by the same three-zone description in 2026
trade coverage.

**OBO's role is not stated.** iMAG says it was "supporting OBO" and worked
"alongside OBO and PUMA"; OBO is presumably the lead agency, but no page
retrieved says so, so the record does not.

**Biometric data went to the screens, not to a record.** Nothing says it was
kept, analysed or attributed to a shoe. That is why Performance Testing was
not assigned.

**A coffee partner reported elsewhere is not recorded.** A research pass
named one; the trade article it was attributed to does not mention it.

**No picture stored.** The images found are a pre-event promotional graphic
and an editorial gallery whose files could not be retrieved.

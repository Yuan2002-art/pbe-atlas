## Description
**A shoe that could only be bought here, for three days.** For the 2025 Boston
Marathon PUMA opened *The High Point* at 745 Boylston Street — "PUMAs ultimate
Boston Marathon experience", in the brand's words — from Friday 18 April. Its
new racing shoe, the Fast-R NITRO Elite 3, was "available to purchase in
limited supply exclusively at 'The High Point'" until the Sunday at 6pm, a week
before it launched worldwide.

**Inside, a lab and a candy counter.** The press described "an immersive
experience that helped attendees get that legendary runner's high", with a
"dedicated NITRO Lab" where visitors could "witness the running economy test…
in real-time… and find out how much time you can shave off your PB". Activity
earned tokens, cashed in at a candy station curated by Madeline's Candy Shop,
and the magazine says the space "saw the unveiling of PUMA's 'Go Wild'
campaign".

**It was the finish line of PUMA's own race.** On the Saturday, "Chase The
High" — which PUMA called "its first ever unsanctioned running experience" —
sent more than 50 runners to three checkpoints across the city, at the Emerald Necklace, MIT's
Killian Court and the Charles River Esplanade, and back to The High Point.

## Verification notes
**The lab is described only by the press.** PUMA's releases name the space,
the address, the exclusive and the university study; the live NITRO Lab and
the candy station are Sneaker Freaker's account. The magazine says it attended
at PUMA's invitation.

**The university is named two ways in one article.** Sneaker Freaker writes
"UMich" and then quotes research "at the University of Massachusetts Amherst";
PUMA's own release says Massachusetts, Amherst. The record follows PUMA.

**"Metres from the finish line" is the magazine's phrase**, not measured here.

**The end date is the exclusive's.** PUMA gives the shoe as exclusive "until
Sunday at 6pm"; no closing date for the space itself was published, so the
record ends on Sunday 20 April.

**A hybrid.** The space is filed pop-up first and product-launch second: it
sold one shoe ahead of its global release, but also hosted a race and a lab.

**No picture stored yet.** The magazine carries photographs of the interior,
not yet requested.

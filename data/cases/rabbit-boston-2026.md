## Description
**A running shop designed with an artist.** For the 2026 Boston Marathon
rabbit set up at 301 Newbury Street from Friday 17 to Tuesday 21 April, and
"collaborated with San Francisco–based runner and artist Zach Litoff to bring
our pop-up space to life." Litoff designed the exclusive rabbit x Litoff
collection sold there, and, per the brand's release, helped design "the Boston
Marathon pop-up space, shaping the in-store environment and activations",
through "custom installations, visual storytelling, and interactive moments"
nodding to "community, creativity, and running as counterculture."

**Five days, each with a job.** Friday: a welcome reception to meet the
artist. Saturday: a 3-mile no-drop shakeout with goodr and Yerba Madre, the
first 75 runners getting sunglasses. Sunday: a second shakeout with HydraPak
and Hyperice. Monday, after the race: free finisher socks, a limited-edition
rabbit x Litoff finisher poster to stamp, a Hyperice recovery lounge with
Normatec boots and massage guns, and finisher portraits, which ran again on
the Tuesday morning.

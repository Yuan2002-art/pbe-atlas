## Description
**A ski brand's shop, running trail tests.** Rossignol's store at 101 Avenue
Michel Croz, in central Chamonix, opened in May 2018 as "a relocation and
expansion project of the former boutique." Rossignol has made skis since 1907
and unveiled its first trail running collection in spring 2025; in 2026 the
store is one of the brand's starting points for testing those shoes on the
ground.

**The room, as the brand described it in 2018.** Designed by the studio L+L
architects, the store has two rooms. The entrance holds "the more sporty
collections"; behind "a wall in tarp plate" is a second "welcoming and
intimate room, with a chimney and display niches", given to après-ski and
footwear. Between them an accessories wall was designed to change with the
seasons, from gloves, helmets and ski masks in winter to "outdoor and trail
products for the summer season." The materials — "weathered larch, slate
stone and black iron" — are those "originally used at the company
headquarters in Saint Jean de Moirans (Grenoble)", and the concept is shared
with the brand's other mono-brand stores.

**The trail test, as the brand scheduled it in 2026.** Rossignol's Run & Try
Days calendar lets runners "test our Vercors, Vezor and Venosk trail running
shoes, directly on the terrain, accompanied by our teams." It lists a *Trail
run test Chamonix* at this boutique seven times: 27 May, 10 June, 24 June,
15 July, 22 July, 19 August and 16 September. The closest to race week was
19 August, five days before it began.

**During UTMB week itself the brand was in the Village.** The same calendar
gives "24.08 - 28.08 - CHAMONIX | Ultra-Trail Village UTMB®" — a stand, which
is not what this record is about. The store is.

## Verification notes
**The description of the room is from 2018 and may no longer be true.** It
comes from the announcement written before the store opened, which gives the
opening as "foreseen on May 13, 2018". That is why the date's precision is
`month`, not `day`. Nothing retrieved says whether the fit-out has changed
since, and the brands the announcement lists alongside Rossignol's own —
Raidlight and Dale of Norway, then recently acquired by the group — are not
repeated here as current stock.

**The Village stand is not a case.** It is one of the stands inside the
Ultra-Trail Village, and Rossignol's own mention of it is a single line of a
calendar. That is the situation the Atlas decided not to write up one stand
at a time; see the Ultra-Trail Village case.

**Two things the listing reports disagree with the brand, and are left out.**
The listing puts a Petzl-partnered test run from the store on 20 August;
Rossignol's calendar gives the Chamonix store test on 19 August and names no
partner. The listing also describes three evening community runs from the
Village stand, partnered by two other brands; Rossignol's page does not
mention them. The listing's own text disagrees with itself about the 20
August meeting point and time.

**Nothing is recorded about any session having taken place.** The calendar is
a schedule, dated 29 April 2026.

**The coordinate is the brand's own.** It comes from the structured data on
Rossignol's store locator page for this address.

**Pictures from two dates.** Two are from the 2018 opening announcement and
show the ski-era fit-out; one, from the store locator, is undated and shows the
trail range. The captions say which is which.

**The two 2018 pictures were re-fetched at full size and resized.** They were
first stored as the 1024px renditions the article page serves. The same two
frames are on the same server as 5811×3916 and 6014×4013 originals, both
carrying 14 May 2018 in their file data — one day after the opening the
announcement gave as "foreseen on May 13, 2018". Those originals were
downloaded and resized once to 2000px wide. The frames are unchanged; only the
resolution is.

**What is not known:** how many runners the tests take, their route or
length, whether the test shoes are sold from the same room, and how the
trail range is shown inside a store laid out in 2018 around skiwear.

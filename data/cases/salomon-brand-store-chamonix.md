## Description
Salomon holds a permanent brand store at 306 Rue du Docteur Paccard in central
Chamonix. The brand's own store page lists foot scanning, custom insoles, ski
boot customisation, rental and repair, and states that the store hosts events
and product tests.

Trade coverage of its redesign and reopening describes a 230 m² space on the Rue
du Dr Paccard shopping strip, laid out by sport — trail running, hiking, road
running, alpine skiing, snowboarding, ski touring — with a separate SportStyle
footwear area, gear repair through a partnership with Green Wolf, and community
activations including athlete appearances, product testing sessions, running
events and mountain safety workshops. Jeff Coquoz is named as operational
partner.

## Strategic purpose
Hold the address permanently instead of renting attention annually — and make
the store's justification service rather than assortment, so that it earns
visits in the fifty-one weeks when no race is happening.

## Archive use
The pilot's control case. Four brands build temporary structures for one week;
this one has a door open all year in the same town. It is what the others are
implicitly choosing not to do, and it exposes what the temporary format is
actually buying.

## Why this location?
Rue du Docteur Paccard is Chamonix's main commercial street — the permanent
retail spine the temporary activations cluster around but do not join. Being on
it means the brand is part of the town's ordinary economy rather than its
event calendar.

## Why this timing?
No timing, and that is the strategy. A permanent store is a bet that continuous
presence compounds more than episodic intensity. During UTMB week it is simply
open, competing for the same footfall without having paid for the week.

## Why this event?
Not event-linked. Notably, the trade coverage connects Salomon in Chamonix to
the Marathon du Mont-Blanc, an event it sponsors, rather than to UTMB — which
suggests the brand's Chamonix strategy is anchored to a race it controls rather
than to the one everyone else converges on.

## Why this product?
The full range across six sports, including winter. The store is not selling a
launch; it is selling the claim that this brand equips everything people do in
this valley, in every season — which no pop-up can assert.

## What performance claim is being reinforced?
Competence rather than advantage. Foot scanning, custom insoles, boot fitting
and repair are claims about knowing the customer's body and keeping equipment
alive — expertise demonstrated through service rather than asserted through
product.

## What cultural meaning is being built?
Local permanence. A brand with an address in Chamonix all year reads as
inhabitant rather than visitor, which is precisely the standing the week-long
activations are trying to rent.

## What role does the physical experience play?
Irreplaceable and unglamorous. Foot scanning, boot customisation and repair are
physical by necessity, not by design choice. This is the one case in the pilot
where the space exists because the service cannot be delivered any other way.

## Experience mechanism
Return visits over years. The mechanism is the relationship: an insole fitting
or a repair creates a reason to come back that no single activation, however
well produced, can manufacture in six days.

## Key strategic insight
Permanent retail competes with event activation on a different axis entirely —
buying depth of relationship with a small local population instead of breadth of
exposure to a large temporary one, and making service, not spectacle, the reason
the space exists.

## Description
**A hotel showroom, the third of Salomon's rooms that week.** For UTMB 2024
Salomon said it would welcome visitors from Monday 26 to Friday 30 August "both
at the Ultra-Trail® Village" and "at the Brand Store Salomon Chamonix" on Rue du
Dr Paccard. Its event agency, Mark Event, adds a third room the brand's page
does not name: a showroom "arranged at the hotel Le Faucigny", where the agency
set out "the products presented" and was "welcoming consumers".

**What else the agency did.** It installed the stand material in the Village,
ran "the brand's activations over the week" and managed "the France ambassador
team".

## Verification notes
**Why this record exists at all.** It rests on one agency sentence, and says
almost nothing about what the showroom was like. It is kept for its address:
two years later, in 2026, rabbit took over the same hotel as its Chamonix
Clubhouse — see that record. The same building housed two brands' race-week
rooms in different editions.

**The brand does not mention the hotel.** Salomon's own page names only the
Village stand and the Brand Store. The showroom is the agency's account alone.

**The brand's page is reused.** It reads "Updated Mar 17, 2025" but still
carries the 2024 edition's text, "from August 26th to September 1st, 2024".
It may be overwritten again.

**Dates are not the showroom's.** The record uses the brand's 26–30 August
welcome, with precision `unknown`, because the agency gives no dates for the
hotel.

**The pin is the hotel.** Geocoded from its name; the 2026 rabbit record gives
the hotel's street address as 118 Place de l'Église.

**The Village stand and the Brand Store are not this record.** The stand is
one of the Village's; the store is its own permanent record.

**No picture stored.** The agency's photographs are not described on its page,
so which room each shows is unknown; none has been requested.

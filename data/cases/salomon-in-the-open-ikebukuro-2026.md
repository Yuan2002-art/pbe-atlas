## Description
**One room.** This record is the SALOMON IN THE OPEN POP-UP at Ikebukuro
PARCO, and nothing else in the Japan campaign. Salomon Japan and the Ikebukuro
PARCO event page both give 23 October–3 November 2026. The room is Main
Building 2F, POP UP SPACE "POOL".

**What the room is for.** The PARCO page describes the capsule on sale from
23 October, and GORE-TEX all-weather footwear. Salomon Japan ties three gifts
to a purchase: a waterproof bag, a limited mug, and a zine.

**The rest of the Japan campaign is context, not this space.** All of the
following is from the same Salomon Japan page.

The launch was one evening, 22 October 2026, 18:00–21:00, at a place the page
gives only as "東京都渋谷区内 某所" — somewhere in Shibuya, not published. The
page says 25 members were to be drawn by lot, and that JISOO does not attend.
The guests, the illustrator and the caterer are not named.

Hankyu Men's Osaka carries the pop-up from 23 October, inside a store pop-up
window the page gives as 30 September–1 December. ISETAN Shinjuku is listed
for 11–24 November.

A stamp rally starts 23 October and runs while supplies last, at four stores
in Tokyo and four in Osaka. The Minami-Aoyama store has a members-only
holding on 23 October, and a zine from 23–25 October. Coffee SUPREME is named
for a members' drink at that store, not at Ikebukuro. The figures on the page
are planned capacities, not results: 150 groups, 300 people, at Minami-Aoyama;
300 zines on the first day and 100 a day on 24 and 25 October.

## Verification notes
**The case boundary is the PARCO room.** The launch, Hankyu Men's Osaka,
ISETAN Shinjuku, the stamp rally and the Minami-Aoyama holding are one
campaign. They are in the description so the room is not mistaken for the
whole of it. They are not collaborators, not a second coordinate, and not
further cases. Coffee SUPREME belongs to the Minami-Aoyama holding and is not
listed under `collaborators`.

**The launch address was never published.** "東京都渋谷区内 某所" is the
page's own wording. No guest list and no production credit were found.

**The pin is the PARCO building, not a street number copied off a page.** No
opened page states a street number. The mall page is filed as `other` because
the source vocabulary has no venue type. Nominatim's building
relation for 池袋パルコ (relation 4853210) sits at 139.7124039, 35.7308314.
The geocoder's own address line is 南池袋一丁目 2, Toshima, 171-8557. That
line is the geocoder's, so it is not written into `venue`. Precision is
`exact` because the hit is the named building the two opened pages name.
`region: Toshima` is the ward on that same hit. The pages say Ikebukuro PARCO;
they were not re-read here for the ward name.

**Three shoe names are not the Japan assortment.** Salomon's Australian
campaign page,
`https://salomon.com.au/blogs/stories-guides/jisoo-steps-out-in-the-open-with-salomon`,
was opened. It names XT-6 Protective, XT-Whisper GORE-TEX and XT-4 OG
GORE-TEX, and it carries remarks from JISOO and from creative director Heikki
Salonen. It also gives an Australia and New Zealand on-sale date of 16
October, which is a different market. None of that is written into `product`
or the description. A line at the bottom of the same page, "SEA LEGS AFTER
PARTY Paris · 100 people attended", reads as a stray site element and is not
used. The page is not a `sources` entry, because this record does not rely
on it.

**Every capacity figure is a plan.** 25, 150 groups, 300 people, 300 zines,
100 a day. No result is published.

**No photograph of the room is stored.** The picture on the Japan page is
captioned "池袋パルコ店のイメージ" — an image of the store, not the
installation. Nothing was downloaded.

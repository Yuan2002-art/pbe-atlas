## Description
**A running shop dressed as a parcel depot.** For London Marathon week 2026
Saucony opened the *RUSH Delivery Depot* at 131 Bethnal Green Road in
Shoreditch — "an immersive public space… that will serve as Saucony's home for
the week". The concept: "RUSH… reframes the marathon journey through the lens
of depots, dispatch routes and long-haul logistics", and "the Depot is built to
deliver endorphins to runners on marathon week" — Endorphin being the shoe
line.

**The fittings follow the metaphor.** Always on: a café; a "marathon mail post
box for messages to loved ones or future selves"; a community wall of
support; a photo booth printing "collectible Fleet ID" cards; an "'Estimated
Time of Delivery' pace band wall"; shoe customisation; and Endorphin shoes to
test and buy. Daily merchandise drops went to "the first 50 visitors through
the door".

**The programme**, per the published schedule: a BRKEDOFF "Delivery Run" and a
launch party with Rinse on Thursday 23 April; a carb-loading evening with
Nell's Kitchen on Friday 24; on Saturday 25 a shakeout run with The Weekend
Project, "The Delivery Network", and a panel, "Make Fast Feel Easy". The first
three were invite only. Hours were 11:00–20:00.

**Beyond the Depot:** a "Race the Delivery Driver" race against cargo bikes
from Saucony's Covent Garden store on Tuesday 21 April, a citywide treasure
hunt, and a finish-line activation "just outside the official marathon
footprint". Those are not this record.

## Verification notes
**The published dates contradict themselves.** The announcement gives the
Depot as "open to all from Thursday 21st April – Saturday 25th April". In 2026,
21 April was a Tuesday, not a Thursday. The Depot's own dated events start on
Thursday 23 April. The record uses 23–25 April, the range both readings
include, rather than choose between them.

**The source is trade press carrying the brand's announcement**, not a Saucony
page; none was found. The wording reads as the brand's release.

**Written from the plan.** The announcement is dated 6 April, before the
Depot opened. Nothing retrieved confirms the week as run.

**No picture stored.** A listings magazine carries two "Depot Environment"
images, published before opening — possibly renders. Their originals are
about 24 and 28 MB each; they have not been requested.

## Description
**A running brand in a cycling brand's clubhouse.** For the 2026 Berlin
Marathon SOAR takes "a four-day Clubhouse residency at Rapha Berlin", from
Friday 25 to Monday 28 September, 10:00 to 19:00 each day.

**At the centre, a vest.** "Our limited-edition Berlin Race Vest", with "a
tightly edited selection of our most advanced race apparel and accessories".
The same vest is also sold at Common Pace, a running store on Hermannstraße.

**One run.** A Friday-morning 10 km progression run, "Starting and finishing at
Rapha Berlin, the pace turns every 2km, moving from 5:30/km down to 3:50/km."
"A city famous for going late. A marathon famous for going fast. We're taking
the morning shift."

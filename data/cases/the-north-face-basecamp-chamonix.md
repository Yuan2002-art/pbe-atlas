## Description
The North Face holds no partnership with UTMB — the organiser's partner list
names HOKA in the footwear position — but it was the event's title sponsor for
twelve years until the partnership was not renewed, announced in November 2014.

During UTMB week 2026 the brand ran a **Basecamp chalet** in Chamonix dedicated
to its Offtrail collection. Trade coverage states that guests were "creators and
European media", who were invited to test the **Offtrail Ultra** shoe and
**Summit Series** products on alpine terrain, and that the chalet combined route
briefings, a product workshop and a recovery space, with coffee service by
Bialetti and equipment from Yeti.

The brand also holds a permanent, **partner-operated** store at 31 Avenue Michel
Croz in Chamonix, open 10:00–19:00 daily, carrying Summit Series and Mountain
Athletics.

## Verification notes
**The 2026 activation rests on one source.** A single French trade round-up
covering eleven brands, which reads as compiled from brand communications. No
second outlet was found, and no official The North Face release. That is why
this record is `partially-verified` rather than `verified`.

**The chalet's address, dates and opening hours are not recorded** — the source
gives none. The pin sits on the permanent store's street instead, and even that
resolved only to Avenue Michel Croz, not to number 31.

**Whether the chalet was open to the public is unknown.** The source names
creators and European media as the audience but does not say whether anyone else
could enter. Recorded as stated, not generalised.

**No agency is credited for 2026.** For the **2025** edition — a different year,
noted here only as context — the Paris agency **HOLD UP!** credits itself with a
week-long Chamonix programme (25–31 August 2025) that was centred on the

course and a Summit Run on 30 August, for ten XPLR Pass members, around the
Flora Alpina collection. That is a self-reported agency case study and it
belongs to 2025, so none of it is recorded as fact about 2026.

**The store's opening date and floor area are not published anywhere** that
could be found, and the store is operated by a partner rather than by the brand.

## Strategic purpose
Stay materially present at an event the brand no longer owns, by hosting the
people who write about it rather than the people who run it.

## Archive use
The Atlas's case of **incumbency lost**. HOKA holds the title The North Face
held for twelve years; ASICS never had one. Three structural positions at one
event in one week is the comparison this pilot was built to make possible.

## Why this location?
A permanent address on Avenue Michel Croz gives the brand standing in the town
independent of the race, and the chalet adds a controlled, private room for the
week. Together they are the two things sponsorship would otherwise provide:
continuous visibility and a space of one's own.

## Why this timing?
Race week concentrates the European outdoor press in one valley. For a brand
without official access, that press concentration — rather than the runners —
is the addressable audience.

## Why this event?
Because it was once theirs. Twelve years of title sponsorship built an
association that outlasts the contract, and continued presence maintains it
cheaply. Absence would confirm the loss; presence blurs it.

## Why this product?
Offtrail is a new line needing a credible first context, and Summit Series
carries the expedition lineage. Testing both on alpine terrain borrows the
mountain's authority for a product the brand cannot yet claim a record for.

## What performance claim is being reinforced?
That the product works on real alpine ground, demonstrated to people who will
describe it to others. The claim is made to intermediaries rather than to
consumers — the weakest link in the chain of proof, and the cheapest.

## What cultural meaning is being built?
Continuity. The chalet, the coffee, the recovery space and the route briefings
compose a hosting posture that reads as *we belong to this valley* — an argument
about permanence, made by a brand whose formal claim to the event has expired.

## What role does the physical experience play?
Central to the mechanism but narrow in reach. A route briefing and a terrain
test cannot be delivered remotely, but only a small invited group experienced
them; the physical layer exists to be relayed, not to be attended.

## Experience mechanism
Hospitality as leverage. Rather than reaching an audience directly, the brand
hosts the people who reach it, converting a small controlled room into published
coverage. This is the only case in the pilot whose primary audience is not the
visitor standing in the space.

## Key strategic insight
A former title sponsor can keep its association with an event without paying for
it — by shifting from hosting the public to hosting the media, and from owning
the perimeter to owning a permanent address just outside it.

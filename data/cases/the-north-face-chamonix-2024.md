## Description
**A UTMB week without the title.** Ahead of UTMB 2024 a Chamonix blog
noted that The North Face was "Not an official sponsor of UTMB anymore, but…
is gearing up and promoting a 'big week' in Chamonix". The brand's answer, as
its agency Chaiten describes it, was to "Place The North Face shop in Chamonix
at the core of the experience".

**One colour for a week.** The campaign was *The Point Of Difference*, "in
collaboration with artist Yves Klein: The IKB (International Klein Blue)
Collection", to "showcase the Summit Series products and the IKB line". The
agency's stated aim: "to paint Chamonix blue with an immersive activation
program and impactful stunts throughout the week."

**What happened in the shop, by purpose.** The agency's plan sorts it into
four:

- "to drive traffic: DJ sets, live race broadcasts, workshops."
- "to boost conversions: live customizations with artist Ombeline."
- "to engage the community: breakfast with athletes / runs with athletes,
  always-on product testing."
- "to create emotional engagement through Yves Klein's unique universe: The
  Blue Run Party."

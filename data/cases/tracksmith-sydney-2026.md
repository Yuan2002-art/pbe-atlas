## Description
**Tracksmith's race-weekend format, in a partner's shop.** For the 2026 Sydney
Marathon Tracksmith set up with "our friends at Up There Athletics" at 21 Foley
Street, Darlinghurst, from Thursday 27 to Monday 31 August.

**Four rituals.** Weekend-only exclusives — "a
limited edition of the Trackhouse Crew and Grayboy Tee" — beside the Sydney '26
collection, which the brand says plays "with shades of Sydney Harbor blue,
gum-tree green, and wattle-gold". An Heirloom Hoodie, "adorned with patches that
represent each marathon", chain-stitched on site — "come back for future
marathons to earn new patches." A free finisher's poster, "hand stamped with
your time", on the Sunday afternoon and Monday morning. And a Saturday 5 km
shakeout with Coffee Supreme and Tempo Journal, food by Common Supply.

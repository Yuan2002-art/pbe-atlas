## Description
**A Boston brand inside a Tokyo select shop.** For the 2025 Tokyo Marathon
Tracksmith took over part of SELECT by BAYCREW'S in Toranomon Hills Station
Tower, from Wednesday 26 February to Monday 3 March — "all weekend long", with
the Tracksmith team in the store.

**The format.** Weekend-only exclusives — "the Tokyo Gloves, a limited edition
of the Trackhouse Crew and Grayboy Tee, and a free gift with purchase that
honors Tokyo '25". A Friday evening kick-off to "get a first look at our pop-up
space". A Saturday 5 km shakeout "local to the pop-up space". And, on race day
and the day after, poster stamping — "one of our classic commemorative posters,
stamped with your finish time" — and the Heirloom Hoodie, "personalized to your
taste with your name or time, as well as patches representing the year and
city of the race."

**What the host added.** The store's own page describes the pop-up as held
with NOAH's support, and the presentation mixing Tracksmith's Tokyo-only
products with NOAH x Tracksmith hoodies and NOAH's spring–summer 2025
collection.

## Verification notes
**The brand's page now carries two years.** The journal page is titled "Tokyo
'25", but below the 2025 schedule it lists a second one — a "Tracksmith x Noah
Kick-off Party… at their clubhouse" on "27 Feb Fri", a shakeout on "28 Feb
Sat". Those weekdays fit 2026, not 2025. Only the first schedule, whose
weekdays match 2025 (Friday 28 February, Saturday 1 March, Sunday 2 March), is
used here.

**One floor or two.** The brand gives "2nd + 3rd Floor"; the host says the
pop-up was on the 3rd floor. The record gives the building and does not settle
the floor.

**The pin is the building.** Toranomon Hills Station Tower was geocoded
directly; precision `exact` for the building, not the floor.

**No picture stored.** The brand's photographs appear to predate the pop-up;
the host's are product shots.

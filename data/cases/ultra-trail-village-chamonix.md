## Description
**The square everyone else rents.** The Ultra-Trail Village is the organiser's
own exhibition on Place du Mont-Blanc, open **24 to 28 August 2026** — Monday
and Tuesday 10:00 to 18:00, Wednesday and Thursday 09:00 to 18:00, Friday 09:00
to 14:00. Inside it, by UTMB's own count, are **125 exhibitors, 110 brands and
7 race organisers**, in front of **50,000 visitors**.

**It is the only case in this Atlas where the space is the product.** Every
other record is a brand building a room to argue for something it sells. Here
the organiser builds the room and sells the floor, and the hundred and ten
brands inside are its customers.

**That figure is the most useful number in the register.** This Atlas
researches individual spaces — a chalet, a shop, a bar, a laboratory — and has
a dozen of them at this edition. The organiser says a hundred and ten brands
were present in one square for five days. Most brand presence at UTMB is not an
independent space at all; it is a stand rented from the race.

**The organiser programmes it too.** The **Fan Experience** returns for a
second edition inside the Village and runs a day longer than the stands do, to
Saturday 29 August: "a vibrant and welcoming space designed for enjoyment,
relaxation, and connection", with live music, race broadcasts, and food and
drink. The stands sell; the Fan Experience is what keeps people in the square
between them.

## Verification notes
**One source, and it is the right one.** Everything above is the organiser's
own page for its own exhibition — dates, daily hours, location, the four key
figures and the Fan Experience description. No third party is needed for any of
it, and none is cited.

**The key figures are the organiser's marketing numbers.** "50,000 visitors,
125 exhibitors, 110 brands, 7 race organizers" appear under a "Key figures"
heading on a page whose purpose is to sell stands. They are recorded as the
organiser's claim, which is what they are, and not as an audited count. The
50,000 in particular is the kind of number an exhibition publishes about
itself.

**Whether the figures are 2026's is not stated.** They sit under the 2026
heading on the 2026 page, but the page does not label them with a year and an
exhibition's key figures are often carried over from the last edition. Treat
them as the scale of the Village rather than as a measurement of this one.

**The Village and the Fan Experience have different end dates**, and that is
the organiser's own inconsistency rather than an error here: the Village is
given as 24-28 August and the Fan Experience, which is inside it, as 24-29
August. Both are recorded as given. The case's date range follows the Village.

**No stand numbers and no exhibitor list are in this record.** The organiser
publishes an Exhibitors Guide as a PDF and a Village map; neither could be read
by this client, so no brand is named here as having been present. The
third-party listing for this edition reports individual stands — La Sportiva,
Merrell, Ronhill, Science in Sport, Sidas, Maurten, Naak, FUGA/Kailas and
others — and those remain reported activity on the edition record rather than
cases. **They are six of a hundred and ten**, and writing each from an
aggregator would add records without adding research. If the Exhibitors Guide
is opened by hand, it names them all at once and properly.

**No images.** Nothing of the Village itself was retrieved.

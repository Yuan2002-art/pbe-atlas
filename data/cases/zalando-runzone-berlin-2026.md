## Description
**A retailer's three-storey house for the weekend.** For the 2026 Berlin
Marathon Zalando opens *RunZone* at The Space, Kurfürstendamm 229, from Friday
25 to Sunday 27 September — "One address, three floors, three days."

**Its pitch.** "They say it takes ten years to become a Berliner. The marathon
does it in 42.195 kilometres. Zalando RunZone is where that starts." Open to
walk into, "the place to move, connect, fuel up, and share the road with
locals, experts, and Berlin's favourite running brands", with answers to "what
to wear, how to move, what to eat, and where to go once you leave".

**Hours:** Friday 7:00–20:00, Saturday 9:00–18:00, and Sunday — race day —
18:00–23:00, after the race.

## Description
**A retailer's three-storey house for the weekend.** For the 2026 Berlin
Marathon Zalando opens *RunZone* at The Space, Kurfürstendamm 229, from Friday
25 to Sunday 27 September — "One address, three floors, three days."

**Its pitch.** "They say it takes ten years to become a Berliner. The marathon
does it in 42.195 kilometres. Zalando RunZone is where that starts." Open to
walk into, "the place to move, connect, fuel up, and share the road with
locals, experts, and Berlin's favourite running brands", with answers to "what
to wear, how to move, what to eat, and where to go once you leave".

**Hours:** Friday 7:00–20:00, Saturday 9:00–18:00, and Sunday — race day —
18:00–23:00, after the race.

## Verification notes
**Two pictures, both of the building and neither of the RunZone.** They come
from the studio that built the container structure at Kurfurstendamm 229, which
dates its own project to 2025. The captions say so. **Nothing from inside
Zalando's three floors has been published**, which is expected: the doors open
on Friday.

**The venue appears under three names** across the sources - "The Space",
"Masumi Space" and "POP Kudamm" - and the photographs here are filed by the
studio under the last of them.

**Zalando's campaign photograph was deliberately not used.** It is a fine
picture of a runner on a street, credited to the brand, but its own embedded
licence reads "15.08.2026 - 31.12.2026 ... PR", and an image whose permission
expires at the end of the year does not belong on a permanent research record.
The city in it is also not identifiable.

**Revisit after 27 September.**

**Announced, not yet open.** Written on 22 September 2026, three days before
it opens.

**Which brands are inside is not recorded.** The microsite says "Berlin's
favourite running brands" without naming them on the page read; its agenda
page was not read.

**Zalando's sponsor status is not recorded.** A research pass reported press
describing Zalando as a main sponsor of the race; that article was not read.

**The Sunday hours are an evening.** 18:00 to 23:00 on race day reads as a
post-race event; the page does not say so, and the record does not either.

**The pin is the address**, geocoded directly.

**No picture stored.**

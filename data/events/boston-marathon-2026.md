The 130th running, on Monday 20 April 2026 — a separate record from the 129th,
because one edition is one record and brands activate at editions, not at
series.

Included as an event record with **no cases yet**. The oldest annual marathon,
with a qualifying standard that makes its field unusually performance-literate.
Brand activity concentrates in the expo and in temporary retail around Boylston
Street rather than on the point-to-point course, which is difficult to occupy.

**What is verified:** the edition number and the date, from the organiser's own
race report. John Korir and Sharon Lokedi both won back to back, and Korir's
2:01:52 was a course record — recorded here only as context for what the
edition was, not as anything this Atlas studies.

**What is not:** everything about brand presence. No activation at this edition
has been researched, and the venue and coordinate are inherited from the 2025
record rather than sourced for 2026.

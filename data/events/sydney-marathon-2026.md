The 26th Sydney Marathon, on Sunday 30 August 2026 — the second running since
the race joined the World Marathon Majors.

Included as an event record with **no cases yet**, and it is the most
interesting of the new road editions for this Atlas because of its name. **The
title sponsor is ASICS**, and ASICS is already in the register as the brand
that has never been a partner at UTMB and built a pop-up there anyway. A brand
holding naming rights at one major and no rights at all at another is exactly
the comparison the Atlas exists to make — but nothing about ASICS's spatial
presence in Sydney has been researched, so that sentence is a reason to look,
not a finding.

**What is verified:** the date and the edition number.

**What is not:** everything else. No venue, because a point-to-point city
course has no single address; no brand activation, because none has been
researched; and no claim here about what ASICS did with its title beyond
holding it.

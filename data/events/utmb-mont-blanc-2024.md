The 2024 edition of the Chamonix finals. Recorded here at minimum depth: it
exists so that cases set in 2024 point at the edition they actually belong to
rather than at a later one.

**What is verified:** the flagship UTMB race ran on 30 August 2024 from
Chamonix over 173.3 km and 9,525 m of ascent, per the organiser's own index
record.

**What is not:** the 26 August – 1 September range for the full week comes from
a search-engine summary of organiser pages rather than from a page retrieved
directly, and the other races of this edition have not been checked. Treat the
week's boundaries as approximate until someone opens the organiser's 2024
archive. This record carries `partially-verified` for that reason.

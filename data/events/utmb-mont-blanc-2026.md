The 2026 edition ran **24–30 August**, with eight races starting from four towns
across three countries and the official Ultra-Trail Village on Place du
Mont-Blanc from 24 to 28 August. The event is formally titled **HOKA UTMB
Mont-Blanc**: HOKA has held the Title Partner position for the series' flagship
events since April 2024, through 2028.

For this research the race matters less than the valley around it. For one week
Chamonix becomes a dense, bounded field where brands differentiate under
identical conditions — same town, same audience, same seven days. It is also a
field with a fence: the title partnership buys the official perimeter, and the
brands without it have to find another way in.

That fence is what makes this edition worth recording in detail. The cases
attached to it include the brand that owns the event's name, a brand that has
never been a partner, and the brand that held the title for twelve years and
lost it.

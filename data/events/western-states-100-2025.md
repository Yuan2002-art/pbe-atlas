The original 100-mile trail race, with a strong ethos around restraint in
commercial presence. Brand activity is comparatively constrained by the event's
own culture, which makes it a valuable control case: what do brands do when
conspicuous activation is socially penalised?

Included as an event record with **no cases yet**.

**What is verified:** the 52nd annual running started on Saturday 28 June 2025
from Palisades Tahoe Resort at Olympic Valley, California, and finished on the
Placer High School track in Auburn, California — all four facts stated on the
organiser's own media release. The venue in this record was previously only
"Olympic Valley start" and carried no date at all.

**What is not:** the end date. A hundred-mile race with an overnight cutoff
plainly finishes on the following day, but the retrieved page gives 28 June and
nothing else, so no `endDate` is recorded rather than one inferred. The point
about commercial restraint is an unsourced reading of the event's culture, not a
finding.

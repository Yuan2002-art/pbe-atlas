The 2026 running, from Olympic Valley on Saturday 27 June into Sunday 28 June.

Included as an event record with **no cases yet**. The original 100-mile trail
race, with a strong ethos around restraint in commercial presence. Brand
activity is comparatively constrained by the event's own culture, which makes
it a valuable control case: what do brands do when conspicuous activation is
socially penalised?

**What is verified:** the dates and the course endpoints, from the organiser's
results page and from independent trail-running coverage. Vincent Bouillard and
Jenn Lichter both set course records — context for the edition, not a subject
of this Atlas. Bouillard is described in that coverage as working part-time as
a shoe engineer at HOKA, which is the kind of detail a case might later want
and is recorded here as reported rather than confirmed by HOKA.

**What is not:** any brand presence at this edition. The point about commercial
restraint is an unsourced reading of the event's culture, carried over from the
2025 record, not a finding.

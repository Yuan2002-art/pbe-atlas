## Why neither lead is a case

- **Arc'teryx IFC, Hong Kong.** No brand-official page was found.
  `arcteryx.com/hk` redirected away from a Hong Kong newsroom. The opening
  date disagrees across the pages that were opened (19, 21 and 23 October are
  all in play, and one of those three is a snippet). Fitz describes an
  expansion and relocation, not a first arrival. There is no related event, so
  `reportedActivity` does not apply either. A case would need a brand source
  and one opening date.
- **On, NYC Marathon Week.** There is no official name for the week, and no
  brand page that lists it. The opened On page is a store listing and does not
  mention the marathon. The sessions are a talk, a workout, a shakeout, a
  cheer zone, portraits and tattoos — activities, not one space with a
  duration. `research/README.md` keeps that kind of listing out of
  `reportedActivity`. They are not added to
  `data/events/new-york-city-marathon-2026.md`.
